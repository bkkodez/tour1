2 Day Uganda Gorilla Trekking Tour from Kigali

This 2-day Uganda gorilla safari offers an opportunity to experience one of Africa’s most memorable wildlife encounters. Travel from Kigali to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, a UNESCO-listed forest known for its endangered mountain gorillas, lush vegetation and beautiful highland scenery. The highlight of the safari is gorilla trekking, where visitors walk through the forest with experienced park guides in search of a gorilla family. It is a rewarding experience for nature lovers who want to encounter mountain gorillas within a short safari.

Key Experiences

Safari Highlights

  • Day 1:

    Pick up and transfer to Bwindi impenetrable national park

  • Day 2:

    Gorilla trekking in Bwindi impenetrable national park and departure to Kigali

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park and Departure to Kigali

On the first day of the 2 days Uganda gorilla trekking tour from Kigali, our driver guide will pick you up from Ruhengeri, brief you about the safari and then start the journey to Bwindi impenetrable national park passing through Cyanika border in Kisoro. On the way you will enjoy great views of rolling hills in Rwanda, terraced farmlands, beautiful scenery among others. Upon arrival in the park you will check into the lodge for dinner and overnight stay at Ichumbi lodge-midrange

DAY 02

Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park and Departure to Kigali

After an early breakfast on the last day of the 2 days Uganda gorilla trekking tour from Kigali, you will check out of the lodge, carry your packed lunch from the lodge and meet up with your driver guide to transfer to the park headquarters for a briefing about the rules and regulations to follow during trekking. After the briefing you will start trekking in the park which takes about 30 minutes to 6 hours depending on where the gorillas are located because they like to move from one place to another in search of food. During trekking you will be able to view other primate species, different plant species, birds among others. Once the mountain gorillas are found you will be allowed to spend one hour with them in their natural habitat which gives you an opportunity to observe their habits, behaviors, watch gorillas feed, breastfeed, groom young ones, take photos and record the breathtaking moments. After the one hour encounter with the gentle giants you will trek back to the park headquarters where you will be rewarded a certificate for participating in gorilla trekking and then meet up with your driver guide to transfer back to Kigali. On the way you will enjoy great views of the Kigezi hills, countryside, terraced farmlands and upon arrival in Kigali your driver guide will drop you off at Kigali international airport for your departure flight or drop you off at your lodge in Kigali depending on your schedule. End of 2 days Uganda gorilla trekking tour from Kigali
