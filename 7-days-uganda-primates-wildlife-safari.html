7 Days Uganda Primates & Wildlife Safari

Embark on a captivating 7-day wildlife and primate odyssey through Uganda's premier southwestern national parks with Roots East Africa Adventure. Journey westward from Entebbe or Kampala to Kibale Forest National Park—the Primate Capital of the World—to track habituated wild chimpanzees and explore the vibrant birdlife of Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary. Descend into the Great Rift Valley for classic savannah game drives across Queen Elizabeth National Park, cruising the hippo-filled Kazinga Channel and seeking rare tree-climbing lions in the southern Ishasha sector. Ascend into the ancient, mist-draped rainforest of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for an unforgettable, life-changing face-to-face encounter with endangered mountain gorillas, followed by serene canoeing and relaxation along the island-dotted shores of Lake Bunyonyi. This well-paced itinerary unites Uganda's most iconic wildlife wonders into one seamless adventure.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Entebbe/Kampala Pickup & Scenic Drive to Kibale Forest National Park

Meet your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide in Entebbe or Kampala after breakfast to begin your 7-day primate and wildlife adventure. Drive westward across Uganda's picturesque landscape, passing traditional homesteads, lush tea estates, and vibrant market towns. Stop in Fort Portal, a lovely town situated beneath the snow-capped peaks of the Rwenzori Mountains, for a hot lunch. Continue to Kibale Forest National Park, celebrated as the primate capital of East Africa with 13 resident primate species. Check into your lodge, relax, and savor a peaceful dinner as twilight falls over the ancient canopy.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest & Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary Guided Walk

Following an early breakfast, report to Kanyanchu Ranger Station for registration and a briefing on chimpanzee tracking etiquette. Head into the dense tropical rainforest with experienced rangers and native trackers. Follow vocal pant-hoots and branch-shakes to find a habituated chimpanzee troop. Spend an unforgettable hour observing them feed, groom, play, and care for their young. After returning to the lodge for lunch, take a guided eco-walk through Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, a vibrant swamp reserve home to eight primate species including red colobus monkeys, as well as over 200 bird species including the iconic Great Blue Turaco. Return to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Scenic Drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

After breakfast, check out and embark on a scenic southward drive toward Queen Elizabeth National Park, crossing the Equator line with spectacular views of the Rwenzori mountain ranges along the horizon. Arrive in time for lunch at your safari lodge overlooking the savannah. In the afternoon, head to Mweya peninsula for a thrilling two-hour boat safari along the famous Kazinga Channel. Sail within meters of massive pods of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, bathing elephants, and extraordinary flocks of waterbirds including pelicans, skimmers, and fish eagles. Conclude with a sunset game drive en route to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Kasenyi Plains Morning Game Drive & Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions Safari

Greet the dawn with an early morning game drive across the Kasenyi plains, the primary mating ground for Uganda kobs. Search for hunting lions, leopards, spotted hyenas, large herds of Cape buffalos, and African elephants grazing in the soft morning light. Return to the lodge for brunch, then travel south to the remote Ishasha sector of the park. Spend the afternoon conducting game drives focused on locating the region's famous tree-climbing lions resting high up in giant sycamore fig trees to escape insects and catch the breeze. Check in to your lodge in Ishasha for dinner and overnight under the stars.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Scenic Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park & Batwa Cultural Visit

After breakfast, take a final morning game drive through Ishasha before embarking on a scenic climb into the rugged highlands of southwestern Uganda. Wind through terraced mountainsides and mist-draped valleys toward Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site and sanctuary to nearly half the world's mountain gorillas. Arrive at your rainforest lodge in time for lunch. In the afternoon, enjoy an enriching cultural visit to the indigenous Batwa community, learning about their ancestral forest lifestyle, traditional medicine, hunting lore, and folk music. Settle into your lodge for dinner and rest ahead of tomorrow's gorilla trek.

DAY 06

Day 6 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Rainforest & Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi

The highlight of your journey begins early with breakfast, collecting your packed lunch, and reporting to park headquarters for a briefing by Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. Venturing into the primeval jungle with expert guides and trackers, traverse tangled vegetation and steep slopes in search of your assigned gorilla family. Trekking duration ranges from 2 to 6 hours. When you locate them, spend an awe-inspiring hour observing these gentle giants in their natural wilderness. Following the trek and receiving your certificate, take a scenic drive to serene Lake Bunyonyi, checking in to your lakeside resort for a relaxing evening and dinner overlooking the water.

DAY 07

Day 7 — Lake Bunyonyi Morning Canoeing & Return Journey to Kampala or Entebbe

Wake to the peaceful mist hovering over Lake Bunyonyi, followed by an early morning dugout canoe ride or boat cruise visiting some of the lake's 29 historic islands. After breakfast, check out and begin your scenic drive back to Kampala or Entebbe. Travel through the rolling hills of Mbarara for lunch, and pause at the Uganda Equator monument in Kayabwe for photographs and souvenir shopping. Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon or evening, where your guide drops you off at your hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight.
