9 Days Rwanda Gorilla, Golden Monkey & Nature Safari

Discover the extraordinary natural wonders of Rwanda on this immersive 9-day primate and nature safari with Roots East Africa Adventure. Spanning the premier conservation landscapes of the Land of a Thousand Hills, this expedition showcases the full spectrum of Rwanda's biodiversity. Begin in Akagera National Park for classic Big Five savannah game drives and a scenic boat cruise on Lake Ihema. Journey south into the primeval montane rainforest of Nyungwe Forest National Park to track wild chimpanzees, walk the suspended treetop canopy walkway, and observe habituated Angolan colobus monkeys. Unwind along the picturesque palm-fringed bays of Lake Kivu before ascending to Volcanoes National Park for an unforgettable mountain gorilla trekking encounter in the bamboo slopes, followed by golden monkey tracking, a visit to the Twin Lakes of Burera and Ruhondo, and an enriching cultural visit to Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival in Kigali, City Tour & Transfer to Akagera National Park

Meet your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide upon arrival at Kigali International Airport or pickup from your hotel. Begin with a brief city tour including the Kigali Genocide Memorial before driving eastward toward Akagera National Park, central Africa's largest protected wetland. Transition from rolling hills to open savannah plains, arriving at your lodge in time for lunch. In the late afternoon, enjoy an introductory sunset game drive across the southern savannah, spotting zebras, giraffes, and impalas before dinner.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Morning Savannah Big Five Game Drive & Afternoon Lake Ihema Boat Cruise

Set out at sunrise for a morning game drive across the savannah plains of Akagera. Search for lions, rhinos, leopards, elephants, buffalos, Masai giraffes, topis, and elands against stunning lake vistas. Return to the lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on a scenic boat cruise along Lake Ihema, Rwanda's second-largest lake. Cruise past large pods of hippos, massive basking Nile crocodiles, and diverse waterbirds including African fish eagles, kingfishers, and rare shoebill storks. Return to the lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Scenic Southern Highway Transfer to Nyungwe Forest National Park

After breakfast, check out and begin a scenic journey southwest toward Nyungwe Forest National Park. Travel through Huye (formerly Butare), stopping to visit the renowned Ethnographic Museum of Rwanda to discover traditional royal regalia, hunting gear, and weaving. Following lunch, continue through rolling tea plantations into the pristine montane rainforest of Nyungwe, one of the oldest rainforests in Africa. Arrive at your lodge in the late afternoon for dinner and rest.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Chimpanzee Tracking in Nyungwe Rainforest & Suspended Canopy Walkway

Wake early at dawn for a chimpanzee tracking briefing before entering the primeval rainforest. Accompanied by experienced guides, track a habituated chimpanzee troop through the dense foliage, listening for their iconic pant-hoots. Spend a thrilling hour watching them leap through giant trees and feed on wild figs. Return to the lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on the famous Nyungwe Canopy Walkway, suspended 70 meters above a deep valley, offering breathtaking panoramic vistas of the forest canopy, butterflies, and birdlife. Dinner and overnight at your lodge.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Habituated Colobus Monkey Tracking & Transfer to Lake Kivu (Karongi)

After breakfast, take a guided nature walk to track a super-troop of habituated Angolan black-and-white colobus monkeys, known for their striking long white capes and playful social dynamics. Afterward, check out and travel north along the scenic shores of Lake Kivu toward the peaceful resort town of Karongi (Kibuye). Check into your lakeside hotel, savor lunch, and spend the afternoon enjoying tranquil views over emerald islands and calm waters. Dinner and overnight by the lake.

DAY 06

Day 6 — Lake Kivu Boat Excursion, Lakeside Relaxation & Transfer to Volcanoes National Park

Enjoy a morning boat cruise across Lake Kivu to visit Napoleon Island, famed for its massive fruit bat colony and panoramic hiking trail, and Peace Island. Return for lunch before continuing your scenic drive northward toward Musanze at the foothills of the Virunga Volcanoes. Arrive at Volcanoes National Park in the afternoon, check into your safari lodge, and relax as the mist descends over the volcanic peaks, with dinner served fireside.

DAY 07

Day 7 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Volcanoes National Park & Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village

Rise early for breakfast and report to the Kinigi park headquarters by 7:00 AM for registration and a briefing by park rangers. Trek into the volcanic bamboo forests with expert trackers to locate your habituated gorilla family. Spend an unforgettable hour standing just meters from the gentle giants, observing maternal care, play, and the watchful presence of the dominant silverback. After lunch, visit the nearby Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village (Gorilla Guardians) for traditional Intore dancing, drumming, and royal cultural demonstrations. Return to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 08

Day 8 — Endangered Golden Monkey Tracking & Scenic Excursion to the Twin Lakes

Return to Kinigi after breakfast for a briefing on tracking rare golden monkeys. Hike into the bamboo slopes to watch these lively primates forage, leap through the canopy, and flash their vibrant golden coats. In the afternoon, take a scenic drive to the volcanic Twin Lakes of Burera and Ruhondo. Marvel at the dramatic reflections of the Virunga volcanoes in the serene waters and visit local fishing communities before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 09

Day 9 — Scenic Return Drive to Kigali, Artisan Craft Shopping & Airport Departure

Savor breakfast with views of the Virunga peaks before beginning your scenic road journey back to Kigali. Arrive in Kigali in time for lunch, and spend the afternoon browsing authentic Rwandan craft cooperatives for handmade baskets and souvenirs. Your driver guide will then transfer you to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight, concluding your grand Rwandan nature safari.
