9 Day Uganda and Rwanda Tour

Experience the ultimate cross-border East African adventure on this 9-day Uganda and Rwanda journey with Roots East Africa Adventure. Commencing in Entebbe, travel across the equator to Queen Elizabeth National Park for big game safari drives and an iconic Kazinga Channel boat cruise, followed by tree-climbing lions in Ishasha. Venture deep into the ancient mist of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a life-changing encounter with wild mountain gorillas. Cross into Rwanda to track endangered golden monkeys in the volcanic bamboo forests of Volcanoes National Park, immerse in royal heritage at Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village, unwind along the scenic shores of Lake Kivu, and explore Kigali's poignant history.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival at Entebbe International Airport & Orientation Briefing

Welcome to Uganda! Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly met by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide and transferred to your boutique hotel in Entebbe. Enjoy a relaxing evening by Lake Victoria with an orientation briefing covering the thrilling cross-border itinerary ahead. Settle in for dinner and a restful night.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Scenic Drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Equator Stopover

Depart after breakfast on a picturesque westward journey across Uganda's scenic countryside. Make a classic stop at the Equator crossing in Kayabwe for photographs and the Coriolis water experiment. Continue through Mbarara for lunch before descending the western arm of the Great Rift Valley, with expansive vistas of tea plantations and crater lakes beneath the Rwenzori Mountains. Arrive at Queen Elizabeth National Park in the afternoon, check in to your safari lodge, and enjoy dinner overlooking the savannah.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Kasenyi Plains Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Safari

Embark at first light on a game drive across the Kasenyi plains, tracking lions, leopards, spotted hyenas, large herds of elephants, and Cape buffalos. Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation. In the afternoon, take a renowned two-hour boat safari along the Kazinga Channel, gliding alongside massive pods of hippos, basking crocodiles, and bathing elephant herds, while spotting pelicans, skimmers, and fish eagles along the shoreline. Return to camp for dinner.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Southern Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions & Scenic Transfer to Bwindi

After breakfast, travel south through the remote Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park. Search the branches of giant fig trees for unique tree-climbing lions resting from the heat, along with topis and kobs. Following lunch en route, ascend into the dramatic terraced highlands of Kigezi toward Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Arrive at your rainforest lodge in the late afternoon, check in, and enjoy dinner while preparing for the gorilla trek.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Rainforest & Border Crossing to Rwanda

Rise early for breakfast, collect your packed lunch, and transfer to park headquarters for a vital briefing by UWA rangers. Venture into Bwindi's misty rainforest with expert trackers, navigating tangled foliage and mountain slopes until you make contact with your habituated gorilla family. Spend an unforgettable hour observing these gentle giants in their wild sanctuary. After receiving your certificate, begin your scenic road journey crossing the border into Rwanda via Cyanika, checking into your lodge in Musanze at the foothills of the Virunga Volcanoes for dinner.

DAY 06

Day 6 — Golden Monkey Tracking in Volcanoes National Park & Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village

Head to Kinigi park headquarters after breakfast for a briefing on tracking endangered golden monkeys. Hike into the bamboo forest zone of Volcanoes National Park and spend an enchanting hour watching these lively primates leap through the branches and forage. In the afternoon, visit the Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village (Gorilla Guardians) to experience traditional Rwandan music, royal Intore dances, and archery before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 07

Day 7 — Scenic Transfer to Lake Kivu & Relaxing Afternoon Beach Excursion

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast before taking a scenic drive southwest toward Gisenyi on the shores of Lake Kivu, one of Africa's Great Lakes. Check into your lakeside resort and savor lunch with panoramic views across the water. Spend the afternoon relaxing on the sand beaches, taking a boat cruise to explore fishing villages, or walking along palm-fringed waterfront avenues. Dinner and overnight at the resort.

DAY 08

Day 8 — Scenic Drive to Kigali & Exploratory Cultural Highlights

After breakfast overlooking Lake Kivu, begin your picturesque drive eastward toward Kigali. Traverse Rwanda's famously terraced hillsides, stopping for photographs and lunch along the way. Arrive in Kigali in the afternoon, check into your hotel, and explore the thriving local culinary scene and coffee shops in Rwanda's clean, vibrant capital city. Dinner and overnight at your hotel.

DAY 09

Day 9 — Kigali City Tour, Genocide Memorial Visit & Airport Departure

On your final day, take a guided tour of Kigali, including an insightful and moving visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial, a testament to Rwanda's resilience and reconciliation. Browse local craft markets such as Caplaki Handicrafts for authentic Rwandan baskets and wood carvings. Following lunch, your driver guide transfers you to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight, concluding an extraordinary two-country journey.
