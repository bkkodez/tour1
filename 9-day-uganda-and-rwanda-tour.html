Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival in Entebbe & Briefing

Arrive at Entebbe International Airport where your Horizon Tours and Travel guide will welcome you. Transfer to your hotel for relaxation and a comprehensive safari briefing ahead of your cross-border expedition.

DAY 02

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

Drive westward passing through green countryside and pausing at the Equator monument. Continue past the Rwenzori foothills into Queen Elizabeth National Park for dinner and overnight.

DAY 03

Morning Game Safari & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Embark on an early morning game drive across the Kasenyi plains tracking lions, elephants, and antelopes. After lunch, enjoy the famous Kazinga Channel boat cruise, viewing abundant wildlife along the water's edge.

DAY 04

Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions & Transfer to Bwindi

Journey south through the Ishasha sector searching for rare tree-climbing lions. Continue climbing into the lush, mountainous gorilla sanctuary of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

DAY 05

Bwindi Mountain Gorilla Trekking & Border Crossing to Rwanda

Trek into Bwindi Forest to spend an unforgettable hour in the presence of mountain gorillas. Afterward, cross the border at Cyanika or Katuna into Rwanda, driving to Musanze near Volcanoes National Park.

DAY 06

Golden Monkey Tracking in Volcanoes National Park

Trek through the bamboo slopes of Volcanoes National Park to observe playful golden monkeys. In the afternoon, visit the Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village to experience authentic Rwandan heritage and royal traditions.

DAY 07

Transfer to Lake Kivu & Lakeside Relaxation

Take a scenic drive to Lake Kivu, one of Africa's Great Lakes. Enjoy an afternoon boat ride on the tranquil waters, explore lake islands, or simply relax along the palm-fringed shore.

DAY 08

Scenic Transfer to Kigali & Cultural Exploration

Take a scenic drive through the rolling hills of Rwanda to Kigali. Explore local cultural markets and relax at your hotel in preparation for departure.

DAY 09

Kigali City Tour & Airport Departure

Tour the Kigali Genocide Memorial and local craft markets before transferring to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
