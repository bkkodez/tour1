3 Days Gorilla Trekking in Rwanda

Embark on an unforgettable 3-day Rwanda primate expedition into the volcanic highlands of Volcanoes National Park with Roots East Africa Adventure. Beginning with a Kigali city tour and a reflective visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial, journey through the picturesque thousand hills of Musanze. Experience the thrill of tracking endangered mountain gorillas across misty bamboo slopes, connect with local Rwandan heritage at the Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village, and track rare golden monkeys in the Virunga canopy before returning to Kigali.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival in Kigali, City Tour & Transfer to Volcanoes National Park

Your journey begins with a warm welcome by your Roots East Africa Adventure guide upon arrival at Kigali International Airport or pickup from your hotel in Kigali. Embark on a fascinating orientation tour of Rwanda's clean, vibrant capital city, including a reflective visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial to honor the country's history and remarkable journey of reconciliation. After enjoying lunch at a local restaurant, take a scenic two-and-a-half-hour drive northwest through rolling emerald hills and terraced farmland toward the foothills of the Virunga Volcanoes. Arrive in the town of Musanze, the gateway to Volcanoes National Park, and check in to your lodge for dinner and an early night's rest.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Volcanoes National Park & Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village

Wake up to an early breakfast and travel to the Kinigi park headquarters by 7:00 AM for registration, tea, and an informative briefing on gorilla trekking protocols. Accompanied by experienced park rangers and native trackers, ascend the bamboo-covered slopes of the volcanic mountains into the gorillas' wild domain. The hike takes anywhere from 1 to 5 hours depending on the troop's location. Upon finding the gorilla family, immerse yourself in one magical hour standing just meters away from these gentle giants. Observe their social interactions, expressive eyes, and foraging habits in silence. Return to the base for lunch and a short rest. In the afternoon, visit the nearby Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village (Gorilla Guardians), where local community members share traditional dances, archery demonstrations, drum performances, and royal Rwandan heritage. Return to your lodge for dinner and overnight.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Golden Monkey Tracking & Return Transfer to Kigali

Following an early morning breakfast, return to Kinigi for a briefing on tracking the rare and endangered golden monkeys. These lively, acrobatic primates inhabit the bamboo zones along the lower slopes of Mount Sabyinyo and Mount Gahinga. Hike into the bamboo groves and spend an enchanting hour watching these vibrant primates leap through the branches, flash their striking golden coats, and forage on bamboo shoots. After the trek, return to your lodge to freshen up and check out, followed by a relaxed lunch. Begin your drive back to Kigali, where your driver guide will transfer you to your hotel or directly to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
