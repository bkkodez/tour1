3 Days Gorilla Trekking in Rwanda

Join our 3 Days Gorilla Trekking in Rwanda safari with Horizon Tours and Travel. Encounter endangered mountain gorillas in Volcanoes National Park with scenic vistas.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

PICK UP AND TRANSFER TO RUHENGERI.

On your first day with us according to this itinerary you will be met by our representative who will pick you up from the airport or your hotel in Kigali. You will be taken for a short city tour where you will visit the Rwanda genocide memorial grounds, some craft shops and your driver guide will give you some information on the culture and history of Rwanda. You will also have lunch depending on your time of arrival. After enjoying Kigali, you will then embark on a journey to your hotel nearer to Volcanoes National park the home of these endangered species to rest and prepare for the trek the next morning. This journey will be approximately 2 and a half hour from Kigali to Ruhengeri. Dinner and overnight at Virunga Inn resort and spa (Luxury)
DAY 02

GORILLA TREK IN VOLCANOES NATIONAL PARK.

As early at 6am, you will be expected to be up for breakfast and ready to set off for the trek. Our guide will meet you after having your breakfast and your lunches packed and he will take you to Kinigi Headquarters where you are expected to be given a brief before starting the gorilla trek. This brief is done at 7am. You are given guidelines during this brief before you are allowed to start the trek. This gorilla trek can take to about 2 hours to half a day as it’s unpredictable. When you finally find the gorillas, you will have to maintain a 6 meters distance and you are allowed one hour to admire and take photos of the gorillas. After the trek, you will return back to the lodge for relaxation and reminisce on the experience or you can ask the guide in case you want to have another activity in the evening where you will go for an optional Ibyi’wachu Cultural tour which is located near the national park. Get to watch dance and singing performances from the local Intore dance troupe. Dinner and overnight will be at the same hotel as the night before at Virunga Inn resort and spa (Luxury)

DAY 03

GOLDEN MONKEY TREKKING AND TRANSFER BACK TO KIGALI.

After an early breakfast on the last day of the 3 days Rwanda gorilla and golden monkey safari, your driver guide will pick you up from the lodge to transfer to the park headquarters for a golden monkey briefing. After the briefing you will start trekking in the jungle in search of the golden monkeys which takes about 30 minutes to 4 hours. During trekking you will be able to view other primates, birds among others and once the golden monkeys are found, you will be allowed to spend one hour with them which gives you a chance to see them jump up in trees, watch them feed, play among others. After the one hour with the golden monkeys you will return to the lodge for lunch and proceed with your drive back to Kigali where your driver guide will drop you at your choice or Kigali international airport for your onward flight back home.
