10 Days Rwanda Wildlife and Great Apes Safari

Embark on the definitive grand circuit of Rwanda on this premier 10-day wildlife and great apes expedition with Roots East Africa Adventure. Traversing the Land of a Thousand Hills from east to west and north, explore the savannah plains and sprawling wetlands of Akagera National Park on Big Five game drives and a Lake Ihema boat safari. Journey through the ancient montane rainforest of Nyungwe to track wild chimpanzees, walk 70 meters high on the suspended canopy walkway, and observe habituated colobus monkey troops. Unwind along the picturesque palm-fringed islands of Lake Kivu before ascending into the misty bamboo slopes of Volcanoes National Park for awe-inspiring encounters with endangered mountain gorillas and acrobatic golden monkeys.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival at Kigali International Airport & Hotel Transfer

Welcome to Rwanda! Upon landing at Kigali International Airport, meet your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide and transfer to your hotel in Kigali. Enjoy an evening orientation briefing detailing the thrilling ten-day wildlife and great ape circuit ahead. Relax and rest well before your journey begins.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Scenic Transfer to Akagera National Park & Afternoon Lake Ihema Boat Cruise

Following breakfast, begin your journey eastward toward Akagera National Park, central Africa's largest protected wetland. Arrive at your safari lodge in time for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on a scenic two-hour boat safari on Lake Ihema, drifting past massive pods of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, and diverse waterbirds including African fish eagles, kingfishers, and herons. Savor dinner overlooking the savannah.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Full-Day Big Five Savannah Game Drive across Northern Akagera

Set out with a packed picnic lunch on an extensive full-day game drive heading northward through Akagera's diverse habitats. Search for lions, black rhinos, leopards, elephants, buffalos, Masai giraffes, zebras, topis, and roan antelopes across the sweeping plains. Return to your lodge in the late afternoon for dinner and fireside drinks.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Transfer to Nyungwe Forest National Park via Huye Ethnographic Museum

After breakfast, check out and journey southwest toward Nyungwe Forest National Park. Stop in Huye to explore the world-class Ethnographic Museum of Rwanda, discovering traditional royal artifacts, pottery, and architecture. Enjoy lunch before ascending into the mist-covered montane rainforest of Nyungwe, home to 13 primate species. Arrive at your forest lodge for dinner.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Chimpanzee Tracking in Nyungwe Rainforest & Suspended Canopy Walkway

Rise at dawn for a chimpanzee tracking briefing before entering the primeval rainforest with expert trackers. Follow vocal pant-hoots to locate a habituated chimpanzee troop and spend an exhilarating hour watching them forage, groom, and leap through the canopy. After lunch, experience the legendary suspended Canopy Walkway, walking 70 meters above a deep valley with breathtaking views of the ancient forest canopy. Dinner at the lodge.

DAY 06

Day 6 — Habituated Colobus Monkey Tracking & Transfer to Lake Kivu (Karongi)

Enjoy a morning nature walk tracking a large troop of habituated Angolan black-and-white colobus monkeys along the forest edge. Afterward, embark on a scenic drive northward along the twisting shoreline of Lake Kivu to the peaceful resort town of Karongi. Check into your lakeside hotel, savor lunch, and spend a relaxing afternoon admiring emerald islands scattered across the shimmering lake. Dinner by the water.

DAY 07

Day 7 — Lake Kivu Boat Excursion, Lakeside Relaxation & Transfer to Volcanoes National Park

Take a morning boat cruise across Lake Kivu visiting Napoleon Island to view its massive fruit bat colony and enjoy panoramic vistas. Return for lunch before continuing your drive north toward Musanze at the base of the Virunga Volcanoes. Arrive at Volcanoes National Park in the afternoon, check into your lodge, and relax as the evening mist gathers around the volcanic peaks.

DAY 08

Day 8 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Volcanoes National Park & Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village

The crown jewel of your safari starts early at Kinigi park headquarters for registration and a ranger briefing. Trek up the volcanic slopes through bamboo groves to find your habituated gorilla family. Spend a profoundly moving hour observing the gentle giants interact, feed, and play in their mountain sanctuary. In the afternoon, visit the Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village (Gorilla Guardians) for traditional drumming, Intore dancing, and royal heritage demonstrations. Dinner at the lodge.

DAY 09

Day 9 — Endangered Golden Monkey Tracking & Exploration of the Twin Lakes

Head back to Kinigi after breakfast to track Rwanda's rare and endangered golden monkeys. Watch these lively, colorful primates swing agilely through the bamboo and forage on the ground. In the afternoon, take a picturesque excursion to the volcanic Twin Lakes of Burera and Ruhondo, admiring dramatic mountain reflections and rural village life before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 10

Day 10 — Scenic Return Drive to Kigali, Artisan Craft Shopping & Airport Departure

After breakfast overlooking the volcanoes, begin your scenic journey back to Kigali. Arrive in Kigali for lunch and visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial alongside local craft cooperatives for authentic souvenirs. Your driver guide will then transfer you to Kigali International Airport for your evening flight departure, concluding your grand Rwanda safari.
