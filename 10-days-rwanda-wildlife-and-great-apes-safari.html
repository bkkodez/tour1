10 Days Rwanda Wildlife and Great Apes Safari

An all-encompassing expedition across the Land of a Thousand Hills. This premier 10-day safari covers Rwanda's complete wildlife circuit: Big Five game drives in Akagera, chimpanzees and canopy walks in Nyungwe, Lake Kivu relaxation, and mountain gorillas in Volcanoes.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival in Kigali & City Tour

Transfer you to your hotel in Kigali.Overnight stay at Kigali Serena (Luxury)

DAY 02

Transfer to Akagera National Park & Ihema Boat Safari

Have breakfast and transfer to Akagera national park where you will arrive in time for lunch at the lodge and later embark on the afternoon boat cruise on Lake Ihema where you will see game like hippopotamuses, Crocodiles and many bird species that live around the lake and the surrounding swamps. Dinner and overnight Ruzizi Tented Camp. Meal Plan: Full Board including Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.

DAY 03

Full Day Game Drive in Akagera National Park

Have breakfast at your lodge and later embark on a full day game drive in the park exploring wildlife in the southern and northern sectors of the park. Search for a variety of wildlife in the park including buffaloes, lions, leopards, giraffes, zebras, rhinos, antelopes, waterbucks and many more. Dinner and accommodation at Ruzizi Tented Camp (Luxury) Meal Plan: Full Board including Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.

DAY 04

Transfer to Kigali and Half Day City Tour

After your breakfast, you will checkout of your lodge and drive to Kigali city. Have a half day tour of Kigali city where you can visit interesting sites like Kigali Genocide Memorial center, Caplaki craft village, Kimironko market, Inema Arts center and Nyamirambo women's village. Dinner and overnight stay at Kigali Serena (Luxury) Meal Plan: Full Board including Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.

DAY 05

Chimpanzee Tracking & Canopy Walk in Nyungwe

DAY 06

Transfer to Volcanoes National Park

Wakeup to an early morning breakfast and report to the park headquarters for briefing and allocation of Gorilla trekking groups. Later head into the cold and misty forested slopes of Virunga national park on your Gorilla trekking journey. The trek can be anywhere between 2 to 6 hours depending on the movement of the Gorillas and upon finding them, you will be allowed 1 hour to see and take photos after which you exit the forest. Meet your driver / guide and transfer back to the lodge. If you feel strong, you may do an optional visit to Karisoke center where you can visit the Dian Fossey museum and her grave site as well. Dinner and overnight stay at 5 Volcanoes Boutique Hotel (Luxury) as above.

DAY 07

Transfer to Nyungwe.

Breakfast will be served early and transfer to Nyungwe national park with enroute lunch. Dinner and overnight stay at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel (Mid-Luxury). Meal Plan: Full Board including Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.

DAY 08

Chimpanzee Tracking, Canopy Walk and Transfer Back to Kigali.

Enjoy breakfast and go for briefing before entering the forest, searching for the active and playful fellows in Nyungwe forest. The Chimpanzees are the easy to spot, as they jump from one tree to another, chattering and laughing. Chimpanzee tracking usually takes about 3 hours. You will then return to your accommodation for lunch. After lunch, checkout of your hotel and head out to the park for a guided nature walk along the Canopy Walkway- a swing bridge 50 meters in length that is suspended from large trees. You will be able to see the amazing rainforest with a bird's eye view, spotting wildlife moving about below you. After the canopy walk you will drive back to Kigali where they driver will take you to the hotel where you will have dinner and rest before your flight out of Kigali. Serena Kigali Hotel (Luxury)

DAY 09

Golden Monkey Tracking & Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village

Trek to observe the lively, photogenic golden monkeys endemic to the Virungas. In the afternoon, visit the Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village to enjoy traditional Intore drumming, dancing, and royal ceremonial demonstrations.

DAY 10

Transfer to Kigali & Airport Departure

After breakfast, enjoy a scenic transfer back to Kigali. Enjoy lunch and last-minute souvenir shopping before your transfer to Kigali International Airport for your scheduled onward flight.
