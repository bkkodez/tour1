5 Days Uganda Kibale and Bwindi Impenetrable National Parks Tour from Entebbe to Kigali

Experience East Africa's two most celebrated primate sanctuaries on an expertly crafted 5-day cross-border safari with Roots East Africa Adventure, starting in Entebbe and concluding in Kigali. Journey west into the lush tropical rainforest of Kibale Forest National Park to track habituated wild chimpanzees and explore the rich avian biodiversity of Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary. Travel southward across rolling highlands with an en-route game viewing drive through the Ishasha sector before entering the ancient misty canopy of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a life-defining encounter with endangered mountain gorillas. Cross seamlessly into Rwanda to experience Kigali's poignant history and vibrant cultural landmarks before your return flight home.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival at Entebbe & Scenic Transfer to Kibale National Park

Meet your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport or morning pickup from your hotel. Embark on a scenic drive westward past lush countryside, bustling trading centers, and rolling tea plantations around the beautiful tourism city of Fort Portal. Enjoy lunch en route with panoramic views of the Rwenzori foothills before proceeding to Kibale Forest National Park, home to the greatest primate diversity in East Africa. Check in to your forest lodge, relax, and savor dinner surrounded by the tranquil sounds of the jungle.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest & Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary Walk

After breakfast, report to the Kanyanchu Ranger Station for registration and a briefing on chimpanzee tracking etiquette. Head into the dense tropical rainforest accompanied by experienced rangers and trackers. Follow pant-hoots and rustling branches to locate a habituated chimpanzee family, spending one thrilling hour observing them feed, play, and groom each other. Other primates you may encounter include L'Hoest's monkeys, red colobus, and grey-cheeked mangabeys. Return to your lodge for lunch, then take an afternoon guided eco-walk through Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, renowned for rare papyrus endemics and the Great Blue Turaco. Dinner and overnight at your lodge.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park via Ishasha Sector

Following breakfast, check out and embark on a scenic southward drive toward Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Route through Queen Elizabeth National Park's southern Ishasha sector, scanning the sprawling branches of giant fig trees for famous tree-climbing lions resting from the heat, along with topis, kobs, and buffalos. Enjoy a picnic or en-route lunch before ascending into the misty Kigezi mountain highlands. Arrive at your lodge bordering Bwindi in the late afternoon, check in, and relax with dinner in preparation for the gorilla trek.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Rainforest & Batwa Community Visit

Wake early for breakfast, collect your packed lunch, and report to the park headquarters for a comprehensive briefing from Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. Join your assigned ranger guide and trackers to trek into the dense undergrowth in search of a habituated mountain gorilla family. The trek takes between 2 to 8 hours depending on where the gorillas nested. Upon locating them, spend an awe-inspiring hour observing their interactions, foraging, and social bonds at close quarters. Afterward, hike back to receive your trekking certificate. In the afternoon, enjoy an optional cultural encounter with the indigenous Batwa community before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Scenic Cross-Border Transfer to Kigali, Orientation Tour & Departure Flight

After breakfast, check out of your lodge and begin your picturesque drive crossing the border from Uganda into Rwanda via the Cyanika or Katuna border post. Soak in the rolling hillside scenery as you travel to Kigali. Upon arrival, take a guided city tour visiting the Kigali Genocide Memorial and local artisan craft centers. Enjoy lunch at a traditional restaurant before your driver guide drops you off at Kigali International Airport for your evening flight departure, concluding an extraordinary primate journey.
