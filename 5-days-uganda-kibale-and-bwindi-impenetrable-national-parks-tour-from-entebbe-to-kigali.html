5 Days Uganda Kibale and Bwindi Impenetrable National Parks Tour from Entebbe to Kigali

The quintessential Uganda safari combining two world-renowned national parks. Experience classic big game safari drives and a Kazinga boat cruise in Queen Elizabeth, followed by an exhilarating mountain gorilla trek in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest.

Key Experiences

Safari Highlights

  • Day 1:

    Arrival at Entebbe International Airport and transfer to Kibale national Park

  • Day 2:

    Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale forest

  • Day 3:

    Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest N.P

  • Day 4:

    Gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable forest NP

  • Day 5:

    Transfer to Kigali.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival at Entebbe International Airport and Transfer to Kibale National Park

You will be met by our company representative in Entebbe with your driver / guide and transfer to Kibale forest heading west past Mityana and continue to Fort Portal where you will stop for lunch. Proceed to Kibale forest arriving in the late afternoon. Dinner and overnight at Chimpanzee forest guest house

DAY 02

Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest

After morning breakfast, wake up early to track chimps near Kanyanchu. This can take you half a day. There are primates in the area like the golden monkey, Lâ€™Hoest monkey, baboons, white and black colobus monkeys, grey checked mangabey, red colobus, blue monkeys and pottos. Other animals include forest elephants unique from the common type, giant forest hogs and a striking tree species. Enjoy Lunch and later take a guided nature walk to Bigodi swamp sanctuary, a good site for birders. The Great Blue Turaco and Papyrus Gonolek can easily be marked among the other bird 137 species. There is also an array of butterfly species. Later return for dinner and overnight at Chimpanzee forest guest house as above.

DAY 03

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest N.P

Early morning, you will meet your driver guide for your transfer to Bwindi impenetrable forest via Ishasha sector that gives you a chance of seeing the tree climbing lions on a good day. Continue to Bwindi with lunch en route and arriving in the evening, check in the lodge and relax. Dinner and overnight stay at Buhoma Haven Lodge

DAY 04

Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest NP

After early morning breakfast, you will head to the park headquarters for briefing, immediately you will be directed to enter the forest with the park rangers leading in search for the gorillas. This will take you about 2-8 hours depending on the movement of the gorilla in the wild, meet and stay with the gorillas for a maximum of one hour. You may have an optional cultural visit of the Batwa people and later connect back to your lodge for dinner and overnight stay at Buhoma Haven Lodge

DAY 05

Transfer to Kigali.

You will have breakfast, check out of your hotel then meet your driver who will transfer you back to Kigali Rwanda. If the city tour was not possible on date of arrival, you may have it on this day. Depending on the time of your flight, you will have lunch and then be dropped at the airport or at your hotel in Kigali as you will advise the driver guide.
