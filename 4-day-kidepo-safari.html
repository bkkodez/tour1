4 Day Kidepo Safari

Venture into Uganda's most untamed and breathtaking wilderness on this 4-day safari to Kidepo Valley National Park with Roots East Africa Adventure. Nestled in the rugged Karamoja region bordering South Sudan and Kenya, Kidepo features vast golden savannahs framed by jagged mountain peaks and granite kopjes. Embark on rewarding game drives across the fertile Narus Valley in search of tree-climbing lions, cheetahs, leopards, and massive herds of Cape buffalos. Explore the scenic Kidepo sand river and Kanangorok Hot Springs, and connect with the vibrant pastoralist heritage of the Karamojong people at a traditional manyatta homestead.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Journey to Kidepo Valley National Park via Gulu & Kitgum

Begin your grand expedition early in the morning as your Roots East Africa Adventure guide picks you up in Kampala or Entebbe. Travel northward through the dramatic landscapes of central and northern Uganda, crossing the Victoria Nile at Karuma Falls. Continue through Gulu and Kitgum, where the scenery gradually transitions into semi-arid savannah and rugged mountain ranges. Arrive in the remote, untouched wilderness of Kidepo Valley National Park in the late afternoon. Check in to your safari lodge, unwind with dinner, and listen to the distinctive night calls of the African wilderness under a blanket of stars.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Extensive Morning & Evening Game Drives in Narus Valley

Greet the morning with an early breakfast before heading out into the Narus Valley, the park’s permanent water basin and a magnet for abundant wildlife. Set against the dramatic backdrop of the Morungole mountain range, traverse the savannah tracks in search of lions basking on granite rock outcrops (kopjes), cheetahs, leopards, bat-eared foxes, zebras, giraffes, Jackson’s hartebeests, and some of Africa’s largest herds of Cape buffalos. Return to the lodge for lunch and to escape the midday heat. Later in the afternoon, embark on an evening game drive as predators become active, soaking in unforgettable panoramic vistas across the valley before returning to the lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Kidepo Valley Safari, Kanangorok Hot Springs & Karamojong Cultural Encounter

Following breakfast, take a scenic drive northward into the wider Kidepo Valley, crossing the dry sand river of the Kidepo lined with Borassus palm trees. This area is known for ostriches, secretary birds, and pygmy falcons. Continue to the Kanangorok Hot Springs near the South Sudan border, enjoying breathtaking views of the rugged mountain frontier. In the afternoon, visit an authentic Karamojong manyatta (homestead). Interact with the warm local community, learn about their pastoralist way of life, traditional homestead architecture, beadwork, and witness energetic cultural dance performances. Return to camp for your final dinner in the wilderness.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Early Morning Sunrise Safari & Return Drive to Kampala

Enjoy one last early morning game drive across the golden savannah plains, capturing final photos of herds grazing in the soft morning light. Return to your lodge for breakfast, pack your luggage, and check out. Commence your journey south back across northern Uganda, stopping in Gulu for lunch. Arrive back in Kampala or Entebbe in the late evening, where your guide drops you off at your hotel or the airport, marking the end of your extraordinary wilderness expedition.
