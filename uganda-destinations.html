Explore Uganda

The Pearl of Africa

Known as the Pearl of Africa, Uganda offers some of the continent's most diverse landscapes and wildlife spectacles. From tracking endangered mountain gorillas in ancient rainforests and chimpanzee encounters in lush jungle canopies to big game safaris across sweeping savannahs and thrilling adventures at the source of the River Nile.

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Home to nearly half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas in a UNESCO-protected ancient mist-shrouded rainforest.

Explore Bwindi

Queen Elizabeth National Park

Famous for its tree-climbing lions in Ishasha, diverse wildlife savannahs, and scenic boat cruises along the Kazinga Channel.

Explore Park

Murchison Falls National Park

Witness the world's most powerful waterfall as the Nile surges through a 7-metre gorge, surrounded by rich savannah wildlife.

Explore Falls

Jinja — Source of the Nile

The adventure capital of East Africa, offering world-class white-water rafting, boat cruises, and the historic source of the River Nile.

Explore Jinja

Kibale National Park

Africa's premier primate destination, home to 13 primate species and over 1,400 habituated chimpanzees for guided tracking.

Explore Kibale

Lake Bunyonyi

Uganda's deepest and most enchanting lake, dotted with 29 emerald islands and surrounded by terraced rolling green hills.

Explore Bunyonyi
