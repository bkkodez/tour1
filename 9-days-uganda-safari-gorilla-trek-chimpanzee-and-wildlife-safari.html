9 Days Uganda Safari, Gorilla Trek, Chimpanzee and Wildlife Safari

Immerse yourself in Uganda's most iconic wildlife and primate sanctuaries on this comprehensive 9-day grand safari with Roots East Africa Adventure. Journey from Entebbe to track wild white rhinos on foot at Ziwa Sanctuary, stand atop the thunderous cascades of Murchison Falls, and cruise the Victoria Nile amidst giant pods of hippos and Nile crocodiles. Continue southwest through the scenic tea terraces of Fort Portal to track habituated wild chimpanzees in Kibale Rainforest, venture across the savannahs of Queen Elizabeth National Park for classic game drives and a Kazinga Channel boat safari, search the Ishasha sector for rare tree-climbing lions, and culminate with a life-changing mountain gorilla trekking encounter in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival at Entebbe International Airport & Hotel Transfer

Upon landing at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly met by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide and transferred to your hotel in Entebbe. Enjoy a relaxed evening on the shores of Lake Victoria with a comprehensive safari briefing from your guide. Rest well ahead of your grand wildlife safari across Uganda.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Transfer to Murchison Falls via Ziwa Rhino Tracking & Top of the Falls

Set off early after breakfast heading north through Luwero and Nakasongola to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary. Embark on a thrilling on-foot walking safari with sanctuary rangers to track wild white rhinos. After lunch at the sanctuary, proceed to Murchison Falls National Park and visit the Top of the Falls to witness the Victoria Nile thundering through a seven-meter gorge before plunging 43 meters below. Check into your safari lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Northern Delta Morning Game Drive & Victoria Nile Boat Safari

Wake for an early morning game drive across the northern savannahs of Murchison Falls. Track Rothschild's giraffes, African elephants, Cape buffalos, Jackson's hartebeests, and prowling lions. Return to your lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on the legendary boat safari up the Victoria Nile toward the foot of Murchison Falls, viewing large pods of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, and diverse waterbirds. Dinner and overnight at the lodge.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Scenic Drive to Kibale Forest National Park via Fort Portal Tea Country

After breakfast, check out and journey southwest past Lake Albert and the Albertine Rift. Pass traditional homesteads and sprawling tea plantations surrounding Fort Portal beneath the Rwenzori Mountains. Enjoy lunch en route in Fort Portal before continuing to Kibale Forest National Park, celebrated worldwide as the primate capital of East Africa with 13 primate species. Settle into your forest lodge for dinner.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest & Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

Gather at Kanyanchu Ranger Station after breakfast for a briefing by UWA rangers before entering the rainforest to track habituated chimpanzees. Spend an exhilarating hour observing them feed, groom, and swing through the canopy. Return to the lodge for lunch, then take a scenic drive southward across the Equator into Queen Elizabeth National Park, taking in views of explosion crater lakes. Check in to your safari lodge for dinner.

DAY 06

Day 6 — Kasenyi Plains Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Set out at first light for a morning game drive across the Kasenyi plains, tracking lions, leopards, spotted hyenas, large herds of elephants, and Cape buffalos. Return to the lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on a two-hour boat safari along the famous Kazinga Channel, cruising alongside massive pods of hippos, crocodiles, and waterbirds including African skimmers and pelicans. Return to camp for dinner.

DAY 07

Day 7 — Southern Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions Safari & Transfer to Bwindi Forest

Following breakfast, check out and drive south through the Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park, scanning mature sycamore fig trees for famous tree-climbing lions resting from the heat, along with topis and buffalos. Enjoy lunch en route before ascending into the terraced highlands of southwestern Uganda toward Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Arrive in the late afternoon, check into your rainforest lodge, and relax with dinner.

DAY 08

Day 8 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park & Batwa Cultural Visit

The pinnacle of your safari begins with an early breakfast, collecting your packed lunch, and reporting to park headquarters for a briefing by UWA rangers. Accompanied by experienced guides and trackers, venture into the dense undergrowth in search of your assigned habituated mountain gorilla family. Spend an unforgettable hour observing their daily interactions, foraging, and social dynamics. Afterward, enjoy an optional Batwa cultural visit before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 09

Day 9 — Scenic Return Journey to Kampala or Entebbe Airport via the Equator

Enjoy breakfast amidst the misty forest before commencing your return journey to Kampala or Entebbe. Travel through the rolling hills of Ankole, stopping in Mbarara for lunch, and pause at the Equator monument in Kayabwe for photographs and craft shopping. Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon, where your driver guide drops you off at your hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight.
