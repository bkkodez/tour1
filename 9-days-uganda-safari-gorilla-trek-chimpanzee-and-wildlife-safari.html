Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival and Hotel Transfer

Arrive at the airport and transfer to the hotel in Kampala. Meet with Achieve Global Safaris and the guides. Dinner and overnight stay at Cassia Lodge.

DAY 02

Kampala City Tour

Have breakfast at leisure and head out for a tour of Kampala city, including a visit to Namugongo Martyrs Shrine, Baha’i Temple, Kasubi Royal Tombs, Gaddafi Mosque, Kabaka’s Lake and the local markets. Have lunch and later transfer back to the hotel to relax for the evening. Dinner and overnight stay at Cassia Lodge.

DAY 03

Kampala to Murchison Falls

Enjoy breakfast at the lodge/hotel and make an early start on your journey to Murchison Falls National Park, with a stopover in Masindi town for lunch. Proceed to Murchison Falls and visit the top of the falls, one of the star attractions in the area, before proceeding to the lodge to unwind for the rest of the evening. Dinner and overnight stay at Pakuba Safari Lodge.

DAY 04

Game Drive and Launch Cruise

Wake up for an early morning breakfast and go for a game drive where you may spot various animal species like elephants, giraffes, lions and Uganda kobs, among others. In the afternoon, go for a launch cruise on the park’s main tourist attraction, the unique Murchison Falls, as well as the River Nile, with its streaming hippos and serried ranges of crocodiles along the sandbanks. You may also see large numbers of different species coming down to drink and bathe. Dinner and overnight stay at the lodge as above.

DAY 05

Murchison Falls to Kibale National Park

Have breakfast and then drive to Kibale Forest National Park, home to the chimpanzees. This is a full-day drive with a lunch stopover en route and a visit to the crater lakes in Fort Portal, arriving at the lodge in the evening. Dinner and overnight stay at Chimpanzee Forest Guest House/Kibale Green Cottages.

DAY 06

Chimpanzee Trekking

After breakfast, go for chimpanzee tracking. Kibale Forest National Park is one of the best safari destinations in Africa for chimpanzee tracking. Kibale’s 795 km² National Park contains one of the most beautiful and varied tracts of tropical forest in Uganda. This is home to a number of forest wildlife species, most especially 13 species of primates, including chimpanzees. After trekking, have lunch and then visit Bigodi Wetland for a rewarding guided nature walk, with sights of different bird species and beautiful butterflies. Return for dinner and overnight stay as above.

DAY 07

Bwindi for Gorillas

Enjoy breakfast in Kibale and transfer to Bwindi National Park, driving through the famous Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park, where you may spot some wildlife, including tree-climbing lions. Proceed to Bwindi with lunch en route, arriving in the evening. Dinner and overnight stay at Ichumbi Gorilla Lodge/Rushaga Gorilla Camp.

DAY 08

Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

Enjoy an early morning breakfast and report to the park headquarters for a briefing by the park ranger guides, after which you head into the forest for gorilla trekking in Bwindi. This could take 4–8 hours, depending on the movements of the mountain gorillas. Upon discovering the mountain gorillas, you will be allowed one hour to view and take photos of these primates, which often seem not to mind the visitors’ attention as they go about their daily lives. Return to the starting point, where you will meet your driver and transfer back to the lodge for dinner and overnight stay as above.

DAY 09

Transfer to Kigali and Departure

Enjoy breakfast in Bwindi and depart the forest, driving to Rwanda’s Kigali city, where you will visit the Genocide Memorial site. Transfer to the airport for your onward flight. End of Safari
