Akagera National Park

Rwanda's Savannah Wilderness & Big Game Sanctuary on Lake Ihema

Plan Your Safari
Back to Rwanda Destinations

Akagera National Park is Rwanda's only savannah wildlife park, covering a diverse expanse of rolling hills, open acacia woodland, sweeping grasslands, and a stunning system of lakes and papyrus swamps along the Akagera River. It represents one of Central Africa's most triumphant conservation revival stories.

About Akagera National Park

Located in eastern Rwanda bordering Tanzania, Akagera National Park offers a striking contrast to the lush mountainous landscapes elsewhere in the country. The park's varied terrain includes low-lying savannah plains, rocky ridges, and Central Africa's largest protected wetland system.

Managed through a successful conservation partnership between the Rwanda Development Board and African Parks, Akagera has restored thriving wildlife populations, transforming the park into a premier destination for traditional game drives and aquatic boat safaris.

Akagera is home to iconic savannah wildlife including lions and African elephants, alongside scenic waterways where boat cruises encounter hippos and crocodiles.

Key Highlights & Savannah Wildlife

Visitors to Akagera enjoy a comprehensive East African safari experience in a scenic and uncrowded setting:

  • Lions & Predators — Apex predators patrol the open plains and acacia thickets, providing exciting game-viewing opportunities.
  • African Elephants — Impressive herds roam the savannah and gather along the park’s serene lake shorelines.
  • Lake Ihema Aquatic Safari — Glide past pods of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, and abundant waterbirds on Rwanda’s second-largest lake.

Major Activities

Exciting safari experiences across savannah and lakes

Game Drives

Morning and late afternoon game drives across the Kilala Plain and savannah woodlands in search of lions, elephants, and antelopes.

Lake Ihema Boat Safari

Cruise the tranquil waters of Lake Ihema for close encounters with hippos, giant crocodiles, and diverse waterbirds.

Night Game Drives

Spot elusive nocturnal predators and creatures under the guidance of expert park rangers and spotlighting gear.

Bird Watching

Discover an exceptional birding habitat holding over 480 recorded bird species across wetlands and savannah.

Prominent Wildlife

Notable wildlife of Akagera National Park

African Elephants

Magnificent savanna elephants that traverse the woodland valleys and drink along the freshwater lakes.

Lions

Reintroduced prides thriving across the northern plains and woodlands of Akagera.
