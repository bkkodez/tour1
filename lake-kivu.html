Lake Kivu

Tranquil Waters, Terraced Emerald Hills & Scenic Island Escapes

Plan Your Safari
Back to Rwanda Destinations

Lake Kivu is one of the African Great Lakes, lying on the border between Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Enclosed by steep, terraced emerald hills and dotted with scenic islands, Lake Kivu offers an idyllic freshwater sanctuary perfect for unwinding after gorilla trekking and wildlife safaris.

About Lake Kivu

Stretching for nearly 90 kilometres along Rwanda's western frontier, Lake Kivu is celebrated for its calm, bilharzia-free waters and dramatic mountain scenery. Picturesque resort towns along its shore — including Rubavu (Gisenyi) in the north and Karongi (Kibuye) in the centre — offer welcoming waterfront promenades and tranquil lodges.

Whether paddling along quiet inlets by kayak, taking a motorized boat cruise to secluded islands, or soaking in breathtaking sunset panoramas over the Virunga volcanoes in the distance, Lake Kivu is Rwanda’s quintessential leisure escape.

Lake Kivu features scenic freshwater beaches, terraced hillsides, and peaceful island boat tours, making it an ideal destination for post-safari relaxation.

Key Highlights & Scenic Landscapes

Lake Kivu provides a wonderful blend of scenic beauty and leisurely outdoor activities:

  • Scenic Freshwater Lake — Pristine, safe waters surrounded by lush tropical vegetation and gentle sandy beaches.
  • Beautiful Hills & Islands — Densely vegetated islands such as Napoleon Island (home to fruit bat colonies) and Amahoro Island.
  • Congo Nile Trail — Famous shoreline trail that winds along the lake's ridge lines through picturesque coffee and tea plantations.

Major Activities

Scenic lake tours and relaxed outdoor experiences

Boat Tours

Take private boat excursions across the tranquil lake to explore historic islands and enjoy panoramic mountain views.

Lakeside Relaxation

Unwind on peaceful freshwater beaches, enjoy waterfront dining, and watch spectacular African sunsets over the lake.

Kayaking & Canoeing

Paddle along gentle shoreline bays and fishing villages with experienced local water guides.

Coffee & Cultural Tours

Visit lakeside artisan coffee cooperatives to discover Rwanda's world-renowned crop from bean to cup.
