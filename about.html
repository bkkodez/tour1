OUR STORY

About Roots East African Adventure

Roots East African Adventure is a Uganda-based tour company founded in 2025 by our co-founder Mr. Kakimba Baker, a long-time tour expert with extensive experience in the tourism industry. Based in Kampala, Uganda, we specialize in creating memorable and authentic travel experiences across Uganda and Rwanda.

We offer gorilla trekking, cultural tours, and extraordinary wildlife safaris, giving travelers the opportunity to explore the incredible wildlife, beautiful landscapes, rich cultures, and unique experiences of Uganda and Rwanda. Our goal is to provide well-organized and memorable adventures that allow every traveler to experience the true beauty of East Africa.

LEADERSHIP

Meet the Person Behind the Journey

Mr. Kakimba Baker

Co-Founder & Director

With over 15 years of experience in the tourism industry, Kakimba Baker has dedicated much of his career to exploring Uganda and introducing travellers to the beauty of East Africa.

Having travelled extensively across all regions of Uganda, as well as Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania, he has developed a deep understanding and appreciation of the region's wildlife, landscapes, cultures, and communities.

Before fully pursuing his passion for tourism, Kakimba worked as a high school teacher. His background in education inspired him to continue teaching, but in a more adventurous and exciting way. He saw tourism as an opportunity to take learning beyond the classroom, allowing people to discover new cultures, connect with nature, experience wildlife, and learn through real-life adventures.

Beyond his professional experience, Kakimba is known for his warm and hospitable nature. He genuinely enjoys meeting people, welcoming visitors, sharing knowledge, and making every traveller feel comfortable and at home.

“For me, tourism is not simply about visiting destinations; it is about creating meaningful experiences, sharing knowledge, and helping people develop a lasting appreciation for the wonders of East Africa.”

— Mr. Kakimba Baker, Co-Founder & Director
