4 Day Queen Elizabeth Safari

Immerse yourself in Uganda's premier savannah destination on an unforgettable 4-day Queen Elizabeth National Park safari with Roots East Africa Adventure. Flanked by the dramatic jagged peaks of the Rwenzori Mountains and the Great Rift Valley, this comprehensive tour features sunrise game drives across the Kasenyi plains tracking lions, leopards, and elephants. Cruise the world-famous Kazinga Channel alongside dense concentrations of hippos and Nile crocodiles, venture south into the wild Ishasha sector to spot legendary tree-climbing lions resting in massive fig trees, and explore the breathtaking Katwe explosion crater lakes.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Journey from Kampala to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Equator

Your safari begins with a morning pickup from your hotel or Entebbe International Airport by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide. Head west along the scenic trans-African highway, stopping at the Equator monument in Kayabwe for fun photographs and geographical demonstrations. Continue through the undulating hills of western Uganda, stopping in Mbarara for lunch. As you descend the western arm of the Great Rift Valley, marvel at the sweeping vistas of the Rwenzori Mountains and volcanic crater basins. Arrive in Queen Elizabeth National Park in the afternoon, check in to your lodge, and enjoy dinner overlooking the savannah.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Kasenyi Plains Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Set out at first light for a thrilling morning game drive across the Kasenyi plains. Follow the tracks in search of lions hunting among the herds of Uganda kobs, spotted hyenas, leopards, giant forest hogs, and large herds of elephants and Cape buffalos. Return to your lodge for lunch and relaxation. In the afternoon, transfer to the Mweya jetty for a two-hour boat safari along the famous Kazinga Channel. Sail within meters of massive schools of hippos, Nile crocodiles, and elephant herds coming down to bathe and drink, while spotting hundreds of waterbirds along the shores. Return to your lodge for dinner and an evening around the campfire.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Southern Ishasha Sector Game Drive in Search of Tree-Climbing Lions

After breakfast, take a scenic game drive southward toward the remote Ishasha sector, celebrated worldwide for its tree-climbing lions. Unlike typical lions, Ishasha’s lions have developed the unique habit of climbing high into the branches of huge sycamore fig trees to escape tsetse flies and enjoy the cooling canopy breeze. Spend the morning and afternoon exploring the savannah tracks, scanning the tree branches for sleeping prides, while also viewing topis, kobs, and buffalos across the landscape. Check in to your lodge in Ishasha for dinner and an overnight stay surrounded by pure wilderness.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Morning Crater Lakes Tour & Return Journey to Kampala or Entebbe

Savor a relaxed morning breakfast followed by a short scenic drive through the Katwe explosion crater lakes, taking in panoramic vistas of the Rift Valley floor and learning about the traditional salt-mining history at Lake Katwe. Afterward, begin your road journey back to Kampala or Entebbe. Enjoy a stop for lunch en route, pausing at traditional drum and craft markets along the way. Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon, with drop-off at your hotel or the airport for your onward travel.
