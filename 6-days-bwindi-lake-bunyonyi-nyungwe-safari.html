6 Days Bwindi Gorilla Trekking, Lake Bunyonyi and Nyungwe Safari from Kigali

Embark on an unforgettable 6-day cross-border East African safari starting and ending conveniently in Kigali, Rwanda. This expedition brings together the ultimate primate encounters and scenic relaxation: venture into Uganda's legendary Bwindi Impenetrable National Park to track endangered mountain gorillas in their mist-shrouded rainforest sanctuary, unwind along the tranquil, island-dotted waters of Lake Bunyonyi—Africa’s second deepest lake—and journey into Rwanda's ancient Nyungwe Forest National Park for exhilarating chimpanzee tracking and the famous suspended canopy walk high above the forest treetops.

Key Experiences

Safari Highlights

  • Day 1:

    Arrival in Kigali, scenic border crossing into Uganda, and transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

  • Day 2:

    Life-changing mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Rainforest and evening Batwa cultural community encounter.

  • Day 3:

    Scenic drive to tranquil Lake Bunyonyi with an afternoon boat cruise exploring historic islands.

  • Day 4:

    Cross border back into Rwanda, traversing rolling tea-covered hills to Nyungwe Forest National Park.

  • Day 5:

    Thrilling chimpanzee tracking in Nyungwe and the suspended rainforest canopy walkway adventure.

  • Day 6:

    Colobus monkey tracking or Gisakura tea plantation tour, followed by a scenic drive back to Kigali.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival in Kigali & Scenic Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Your safari begins with a warm welcome by your professional Horizon Tours and Travel driver-guide at Kigali International Airport or your Kigali hotel. After a short safari briefing and an optional visit to the historic Kigali Genocide Memorial, embark on a picturesque northward journey through Rwanda’s terraced hillside landscapes. Cross the border into southwestern Uganda via the Katuna or Cyanika border post, continuing through the mist-capped Kigezi highlands to your safari lodge bordering Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Enjoy an evening at leisure surrounded by the sounds of the ancient rainforest.

DAY 02

Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi & Community Cultural Experience

Following an early morning breakfast, report to the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) park headquarters for an essential ranger briefing on gorilla trekking etiquette and forest conservation. Led by expert trackers and armed rangers, venture into the dense tropical rainforest in search of your assigned habituated mountain gorilla family. Treks can range between 2 to 6 hours depending on where the family nested the previous night. Once located, spend an awe-inspiring, unforgettable 60 minutes observing the silverback, females, and energetic young gorillas playing and foraging just meters away. Return to the lodge for a celebratory certificate presentation, followed by an afternoon Batwa or Bakiga community cultural interaction.

Accommodation: [Enter Lodge / Hotel Name Here - Luxury / Mid-range / Budget]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Packed Lunch & Dinner

DAY 03

Scenic Drive to Lake Bunyonyi & Afternoon Island Canoe Exploration

After a leisurely breakfast with mountain mist rising above the Bwindi canopy, depart for Lake Bunyonyi—renowned as one of the safest and most picturesque freshwater lakes in East Africa. Nestled among steep terraced hills, Lake Bunyonyi features 29 lush islands. Arrive in time for lunch at your lakeside retreat. In the afternoon, embark on a scenic motorized boat cruise or traditional dugout canoe tour to discover the rich history of Akampene (Punishment Island), Sharp’s Island, and local island community schools. Spend the evening relaxing on your balcony with serene sunset lake views.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 04

Cross Border to Rwanda & Transfer to Nyungwe Forest National Park

Savor an early morning breakfast over the tranquil lake waters before checking out. Begin your cross-border journey back into Rwanda, traveling through western Rwanda’s rolling green highlands. The scenic drive offers breathtaking vistas of Lake Kivu, vibrant local market towns, and emerald-green tea plantations contouring the hillsides. Arrive in the afternoon at Nyungwe Forest National Park—one of the largest and best-preserved montane rainforest tracts in Central Africa. Check in to your lodge and relax amidst the fresh mountain air.

Accommodation: [Enter Nyungwe Lodge / Hotel Name Here]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 05

Chimpanzee Tracking in Nyungwe Forest & Suspended Canopy Walkway

An early dawn start takes you to the park headquarters at Uwinka or Cyamudongo for a pre-trek briefing. Enter Nyungwe’s primary montane rainforest to track habituated wild chimpanzees. Listen for their vocal pant-hoots echoing through the dense canopy as you follow their movements through the trees. After spending a thrilling hour observing chimpanzee troop dynamics, return to the lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on the world-famous Nyungwe Canopy Walkway—a suspension bridge hanging 70 meters above the forest floor—providing spectacular panoramic views of ancient tree canopies, rare orchids, and exotic forest bird species.

Accommodation: [Enter Nyungwe Lodge / Hotel Name Here]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 06

Colobus Monkey Walk / Tea Estate Tour & Return Transfer to Kigali

Enjoy a hearty breakfast before undertaking a guided nature walk to encounter large semi-habituated troops of Angolan colobus monkeys in Gisakura, or take a fascinating cultural tour of a working tea estate to see how premium Rwandan tea is harvested and processed. After lunch, begin the scenic drive back to Kigali. Arrive in the late afternoon or early evening for drop-off at your hotel or direct transfer to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight, concluding your 6-day cross-border East African adventure.

Meal Plan: Breakfast & Lunch

Drop-off: Kigali Airport or Hotel
