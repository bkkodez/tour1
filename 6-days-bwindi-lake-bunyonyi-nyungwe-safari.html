6 Days Bwindi Gorilla Trekking, Lake Bunyonyi and Nyungwe Safari from Kigali

Embark on an unforgettable 6-day cross-border East African safari starting and ending conveniently in Kigali, Rwanda. This expedition brings together the ultimate primate encounters and scenic relaxation: venture into Uganda's legendary Bwindi Impenetrable National Park to track endangered mountain gorillas in their mist-shrouded rainforest sanctuary, unwind along the tranquil, island-dotted waters of Lake Bunyonyi—Africa’s second deepest lake—and journey into Rwanda's ancient Nyungwe Forest National Park for exhilarating chimpanzee tracking and the famous suspended canopy walk high above the forest treetops.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 â€” Arrival in Kigali, Scenic Border Crossing & Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Your safari begins with a morning pickup from Kigali International Airport or your hotel in Kigali by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide. Following an orientation briefing, take a scenic drive northward through Rwanda's rolling emerald hills, crossing into southwestern Uganda via the Cyanika or Katuna border post. Traverse the dramatic terraced highlands of Kigezi, often called the 'Switzerland of Africa', arriving at your forest lodge bordering Bwindi Impenetrable National Park in the afternoon. Settle in, enjoy dinner, and rest ahead of tomorrow's gorilla trek.

DAY 02

Day 2 â€” Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Rainforest & Batwa Cultural Community Experience

Rise early for breakfast, collect your packed lunch, and head to the park headquarters for a vital briefing on gorilla etiquette and tracking safety from Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. In the company of expert trackers, journey into Bwindi's dense jungle canopy. Tracking can take from 2 to 6 hours depending on the troop's location. Once found, spend a deeply moving hour observing the magnificent mountain gorillas feeding, playing, and communicating in their wild habitat. In the late afternoon, enjoy an authentic Batwa cultural community encounter, discovering their ancient forest survival lore and traditional dances before returning to the lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 â€” Scenic Drive to Lake Bunyonyi & Afternoon Island Canoe Exploration

After breakfast, take a scenic drive through the highland hills toward picturesque Lake Bunyonyi, one of Africa's deepest, most serene freshwater lakes dotted with 29 lush islands. Check in to your lakeside resort and enjoy lunch overlooking the calm waters. In the afternoon, set out on a motorized boat or traditional dugout canoe tour exploring historical islands, including Akampene (Punishment Island) and Bwama Island. Enjoy swimming in the bilharzia-free waters, birdwatching, and a relaxing evening admiring the sunset over the hills.

DAY 04

Day 4 â€” Cross Border to Rwanda & Scenic Transfer to Nyungwe Forest National Park

Savor an early breakfast before beginning your journey back across the border into Rwanda via Katuna or Cyanika. Travel south and west through Rwanda's breathtaking highland landscapes, passing rolling green tea plantations and picturesque rural villages. Continue past the southern shores of Lake Kivu toward Nyungwe Forest National Park, one of the oldest montane rainforests in Central Africa and home to 13 primate species. Arrive at your lodge in the late afternoon for dinner and an early night.

DAY 05

Day 5 â€” Chimpanzee Tracking in Nyungwe Forest & Suspended Rainforest Canopy Walkway

Wake early at dawn and transfer to the park visitor center for a chimpanzee tracking briefing before entering the primeval rainforest. Accompanied by experienced guides, track a habituated chimpanzee community, listening for their loud vocalizations echoing through the giant tree canopy. Spend an exhilarating hour watching them leap between branches and forage for wild fruit. Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation. In the afternoon, embark on the legendary Nyungwe Canopy Walkway, suspended 70 meters above a deep ravine, offering panoramic views over the ancient forest canopy, birds, and primates.

DAY 06

Day 6 â€” Colobus Monkey Tracking or Gisakura Tea Estate Tour & Scenic Transfer to Kigali

Following breakfast, enjoy a morning walk to track a large troop of habituated Angolan black-and-white colobus monkeys, known for their striking long white capes, or take an insightful guided tour of the Gisakura Tea Estate to discover tea harvesting and processing. Afterward, commence your scenic drive back to Kigali, stopping at the King's Palace Museum in Nyanza or the Ethnographic Museum in Huye for lunch and cultural history. Arrive in Kigali in the late afternoon for drop-off at your hotel or Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
