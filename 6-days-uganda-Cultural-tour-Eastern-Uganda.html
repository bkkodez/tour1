6 Days Uganda Gorilla Trekking Safari in Uganda from Kigali

Take advantage of the shortest and most scenic route into Uganda's premier wildlife sanctuaries on this exceptional 6-day safari starting and ending in Kigali, Rwanda. By arriving into Kigali and crossing directly into southwestern Uganda, you dramatically reduce overland road travel while combining two world-class national parks: tracking wild mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, experiencing classic savannah wildlife, tree-climbing lions, and a Kazinga Channel boat safari in Queen Elizabeth National Park, and unwinding along the picturesque island shores of Lake Bunyonyi.

Key Experiences

Safari Highlights

  • Day 1:

    Kigali airport pick-up, scenic border crossing into Uganda, and transfer to Bwindi forest lodge.

  • Day 2:

    Exhilarating mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park with 1 hour of close observation.

  • Day 3:

    Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Ishasha searching for unique tree-climbing lions.

  • Day 4:

    Morning Kasenyi savannah wildlife game drive and afternoon boat safari on the famous Kazinga Channel.

  • Day 5:

    Scenic explosion crater lakes drive and transfer to Lake Bunyonyi for afternoon island boat cruise.

  • Day 6:

    Relaxed lakefront breakfast, scenic return journey to Kigali, and departure airport drop-off.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Kigali Pick-up & Scenic Cross-Border Transfer to Bwindi (Uganda)

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport or pickup from your hotel in Kigali, you will be warmly received by your Horizon Tours and Travel safari driver-guide. Embark on a brief orientation tour of Kigali, including an optional visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial. Begin the scenic 4-hour drive northward through the rolling hills of Rwanda, crossing into Uganda via the Cyanika or Katuna border post. Continue through the undulating terraced green slopes of the Kigezi highlands to your lodge bordering Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Enjoy a tranquil evening taking in the misty mountain atmosphere and preparing for tomorrow’s gorilla trek.

DAY 02

Thrilling Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Following an early breakfast, transfer to the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) park headquarters for registration and a comprehensive briefing on gorilla trekking guidelines. Enter the primeval rainforest alongside expert trackers and armed rangers. Trekking through Bwindi’s rugged, tangled vegetation may take between 2 to 6 hours depending on the movement of the gorillas. The moment you find the gorilla troop is truly magical: spend one sacred hour observing the mighty silverback watching over his family, mothers nursing babies, and juveniles playing in the trees. After receiving your official gorilla tracking certificate, return to your lodge for dinner and fireside relaxation.

Accommodation: [Enter Bwindi Lodge / Camp Name Here - Luxury / Mid-range / Budget]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Packed Lunch & Dinner

DAY 03

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions

After breakfast, depart Bwindi and drive northward into Queen Elizabeth National Park, Uganda’s most famous savannah wildlife destination. Enter the park via the southern Ishasha sector, world-renowned for its rare population of tree-climbing lions. Enjoy a game drive through Ishasha scanning the branches of ancient sycamore fig trees where lions regularly lounge to escape the heat and tsetse flies. Continue through the park, spotting Uganda kobs, topis, and elephants, and arrive at your lodge in the central sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park in time for dinner with sweeping views over the African plains.

Accommodation: [Enter Queen Elizabeth Safari Lodge Name Here - Luxury / Mid-range / Budget]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 04

Kasenyi Savannah Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Rise early for a dawn game drive across the Kasenyi plains where savannah predators are at their most active. Track lions, leopards, and spotted hyenas, alongside large herds of African elephants, cape buffaloes, defassa waterbucks, and warthogs. Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation. In the afternoon, embark on the renowned 2-hour boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel—a natural 32-kilometer waterway connecting Lake George and Lake Edward. This boat safari offers unparalleled close-up views of the world's densest concentration of hippopotamus, massive Nile crocodiles basking on sandbanks, bathing elephant herds, and hundreds of vibrant waterbird species including pink-backed pelicans and African skimmers.

Accommodation: [Enter Queen Elizabeth Safari Lodge Name Here - Luxury / Mid-range / Budget]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 05

Scenic Explosion Crater Lakes Drive & Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi

After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive through the Katwe explosion crater lakes area, offering breathtaking panorama vistas of ancient volcanic craters and the Rwenzori mountain ranges. Depart Queen Elizabeth National Park and travel southward toward serene Lake Bunyonyi—one of Africa's deepest and most tranquil crater lakes, framed by 29 lush islands. Arrive at your lakeside eco-lodge in time for lunch. In the afternoon, set out on a motorized boat trip across the lake visiting historical islands such as Akampene (Punishment Island) and Bwama Island, or simply relax at the water's edge enjoying the calm mountain scenery.

Accommodation: [Enter Lake Bunyonyi Resort / Eco-Lodge Name Here - Luxury / Mid-range / Budget]

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 06

Lake Bunyonyi Sunrise Relaxation & Return Transfer to Kigali

Wake up to a peaceful sunrise with morning mist hovering over Lake Bunyonyi's glass-like waters. After a leisurely breakfast on the terrace, check out and begin your return journey back across the border into Rwanda. The scenic drive winds back into Kigali by afternoon. Depending on your flight departure schedule, visit local Kigali artisan craft centers for souvenirs before your final transfer to Kigali International Airport for your flight home, concluding an extraordinary 6-day Uganda gorilla and wildlife journey.

Meal Plan: Breakfast & Lunch

Drop-off: Kigali International Airport or Kigali Hotel
