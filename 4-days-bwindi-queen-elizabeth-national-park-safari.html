4 Days Bwindi & Queen Elizabeth National Park Safari

Combine two of Uganda's most celebrated national parks on this exhilarating 4-day wildlife and primate expedition with Roots East Africa Adventure. Journey southwest from Kampala or Entebbe across the Equator into the savannahs of Queen Elizabeth National Park. Experience thrilling game drives across the Kasenyi plains in search of lions, leopards, elephants, and giant forest hogs, and take a panoramic boat safari along the Kazinga Channel amid dense pods of hippos and basking crocodiles. Ascend into the ancient, misty rainforests of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a once-in-a-lifetime face-to-face encounter with endangered mountain gorillas in their untamed natural habitat, complemented by an authentic Batwa cultural community experience. This compact safari delivers the quintessential Ugandan big game and great ape safari.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Transfer from Kampala/Entebbe to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Equator

Meet your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide in Kampala or Entebbe in the morning to start your four-day wildlife and gorilla expedition. Journey southwest through Uganda's scenic countryside, pausing at the Equator marker in Kayabwe for photographs and the Coriolis water experiment. Continue through Mbarara for lunch, then journey through lush tea plantations and rolling hills descending into the Western Rift Valley. Arrive at Queen Elizabeth National Park in the late afternoon, enjoying an introductory game drive as you head to your lodge for dinner and overnight.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Morning Savannah Game Drive, Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise & Transfer to Bwindi

Begin the day with an early morning game drive across the Kasenyi plains. Track lions, leopards, elephants, buffalos, and abundant herds of Uganda kob in the morning light. Return to the lodge for an early lunch, then head to Mweya peninsula for a two-hour boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel. Marvel at one of Africa’s highest concentrations of hippos, along with basking crocodiles and diverse waterbirds. Following the cruise, embark on a scenic drive southward through the Ishasha sector or via the rolling hills of Rukungiri, arriving at Bwindi Impenetrable National Park in the evening. Check in to your forest lodge for dinner and rest.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park & Batwa Cultural Visit

Rise early for breakfast, collect your packed lunch, and report to the park headquarters for a briefing on gorilla trekking guidelines. Led by experienced Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers and expert trackers, venture into the dense, misty rainforest. The hike can last between 2 and 6 hours over rugged mountain terrain depending on the location of the gorilla family. Once you find them, enjoy one unforgettable hour observing these magnificent creatures in their natural habitat. Watch the silverback safeguard the family as females nurse and playful youngsters romp through the canopy. Return to the park center to receive your trekking certificate. In the late afternoon, take part in an optional cultural encounter with the indigenous Batwa community, discovering their ancient hunter-gatherer traditions and music before dinner at the lodge.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Scenic Drive Back to Kampala, Entebbe, or Kigali

After breakfast, begin your return journey with fond memories of an extraordinary safari. Depending on your flight schedule and departure point, travel back to Kampala/Entebbe with stops for lunch and craft shopping along the Equator highway, or take a scenic drive south across the border to Kigali, Rwanda, for your evening flight departure from Kigali International Airport.
