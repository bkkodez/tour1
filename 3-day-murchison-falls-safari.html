3 Day Murchison falls Safari

Embark on an exhilarating 3-day wildlife adventure to Murchison Falls National Park, Uganda's largest and most dramatic conservation sanctuary, with Roots East Africa Adventure. Track wild southern white rhinos on foot at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary before entering the park to witness the explosive power of the Victoria Nile as it roars through a narrow seven-meter gorge. Explore the northern savannahs on sunrise game drives in search of lions, leopards, Rothschild's giraffes, and elephants, and cruise the calm waters of the Nile up to the base of the thundering falls amidst basking crocodiles and pods of hippos.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Journey to Murchison Falls National Park via Ziwa Rhino Tracking & Top of the Falls

Following an early morning briefing in Kampala or Entebbe, travel northward through the vibrant towns and lush agricultural landscapes of central Uganda. Your first major stop is the Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary in Nakasongola, the proud home of Uganda's wild white rhino reintroduction program. Here, embark on a guided walking safari with sanctuary rangers to track these prehistoric mammals on foot at a safe, thrilling distance. Continue with lunch at the sanctuary before journeying onward to Murchison Falls National Park. Upon entering the park, ascend to the dramatic Top of the Falls, where the entire Victoria Nile crashes with deafening power through a narrow seven-meter rock gorge before tumbling 43 meters into the pool below. After taking in the misty spray and rainbow formations, cross over to your safari lodge for dinner and a restful evening.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Morning Savannah Game Drive & Afternoon River Nile Boat Cruise

Awaken at sunrise for an exhilarating morning game drive across the sweeping savannahs of the northern delta sector. With golden morning light flooding the plains, search for Rothschild’s giraffes, African elephants, Cape buffalos, Jackson’s hartebeests, oribi, Uganda kobs, and prowling predators including lions and leopards. Return to your lodge for lunch and relaxation. In the afternoon, head to the Paraa jetty for a legendary three-hour boat safari upriver toward the foot of Murchison Falls. Glide alongside basking Nile crocodiles, submerged pods of hippos, and abundant birdlife including African fish eagles, goliath herons, and kingfishers. As the boat nears the roaring base of the falls, marvel at the wall of churning white water before returning to the dock and enjoying an evening sundowner back at camp.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Final Morning Game Safari & Return Drive to Kampala or Entebbe

Savor a relaxed breakfast amidst the peaceful morning sounds of the African bush. Set out on a scenic departure game drive through the park’s southern woodland and savannah, keeping a lookout for any elusive wildlife species you may have missed. Exit the park and commence your comfortable road trip back south through Masindi and the scenic countryside. Enjoy a stop for lunch en route, pausing at roadside craft markets to browse handmade Ugandan souvenirs. Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon or early evening, where your guide drops you off at your hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your flight home.
