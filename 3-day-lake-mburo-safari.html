3 Days Queen Elizabeth National Park Safari

The 3 days Queen Elizabeth national park safari will take you to southwestern region to Queen Elizabeth national park which is the second largest national park in Uganda after Murchison falls national park. The national park harbors a wide range of wildlife species including the big 4 mammals which are the lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards, other animals like waterbucks, warthogs, bushbucks, giant forest hogs, hippos, Uganda kobs among others which will be viewed during the game drives and boat cruise on Kazinga channel.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Pick up and transfer to Queen Elizabeth national park

Pick up and transfer to Queen Elizabeth national park, depending on your location on the first day of the 3 days Queen Elizabeth national park safari you will be welcomed by our driver guide who will brief you about the safari and then start the journey to Queen Elizabeth national park. On the way you will have a stopover at the Equator in Kayabwe where you will be able to take photos, buy souvenirs and then continue the journey with another stopover in Mbarara town for lunch. After lunch you will continue the journey with great views of tea plantations, crater lakes, Rwenzori Mountains, beautiful landscapes among others and upon arrival you will drive through the park to the lodge for dinner and overnight.

DAY 02

Morning game drive and afternoon boat cruise

Morning game drive: after an early breakfast on the second day of the 3 days Queen Elizabeth national park safari you will meet up with your driver to go for a morning game drive early morning. During the game drive which is commonly done in Kasenyi plains you will be able to view different animals such as elephants, buffaloes, waterbucks, bushbucks, warthogs, view predators like lions and leopards if you are lucky, view different bird species like grey crowned crane, red throat spur fowl, martial eagle, black bellied bustard, Senegal plover, white riled lark, among others, take beautiful pictures of the landscape, sunrise among others. After the game drive you will drive back to the lodge for lunch.
Boat cruise: after lunch you will drive to the landing site near the luxurious lodge Mweya safari lodge for an exciting boat cruise on Kazinga channel, a water body that joins Lake Edward and Lake George. The scheduled boat cruise takes about 2 to 3 hours and gives you an opportunity take clear photos, cruise meters away from large concentrations of hippos and Nile crocodiles in water, other animals like elephants, buffaloes, antelopes among others.View fishermen on the lake shores preparing their fishing gears for night fishing among others. After the interesting boat cruise you will meet up with your driver guide and go for an evening game drive where you will view different animals going back to their resting areas. After the game drive you will return to the lodge for dinner and overnight.

DAY 03

Crater lakes tour and departure to Kampala

Crater lakes tour: after a relaxed breakfast on the last day of the 3 days Queen Elizabeth national park safari you will check out of the lodge and meet up with your driver to go for an optional crater lakes tour. During the tour you will visit Katwe Crater Lake where you will be able to interact with the local people who will be extracting rock salt from the lake, view different mammals and bird species and then connect to Nyamunuka crater lake which is also known as the hospital for animals. After the Crater Lakes tour you will drive out of the park and start the journey to Kampala with en-route lunch. After lunch you will continue the journey to Kampala arriving in the evening and upon arrival your driver guide will drop you off at any place of your choice.
