3 Days Queen Elizabeth National Park Safari

Discover the captivating wilderness of Queen Elizabeth National Park on an immersive 3-day safari with Roots East Africa Adventure. Traversing the scenic equator circuit into Uganda's Great Rift Valley, embark on sunrise game drives across the Kasenyi plains tracking lions, elephants, and leopards, cruise the world-renowned Kazinga Channel alongside immense pods of hippos and basking crocodiles, and explore the ancient volcanic Katwe explosion crater lakes.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Journey to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Equator Stopover

Your safari starts with a morning pickup from your hotel or residence in Kampala or Entebbe by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide. Head southwest through the Ugandan countryside, stopping at the Equator in Kayabwe for photography and water-flow Coriolis demonstrations. Continue through Masaka and the rolling hills of Ankole, arriving in Mbarara for lunch. In the afternoon, travel past scenic tea plantations and lush crater landscapes as you descend into the Western Rift Valley. Arrive at Queen Elizabeth National Park in time for an evening arrival game drive on your way to your safari lodge, where dinner and a peaceful night await.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Kasenyi Plains Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Set out at dawn onto the vast savannah of the Kasenyi sector, the park's primary breeding ground for Uganda kobs. The open plains offer excellent opportunities to spot prowling lions, elusive leopards, spotted hyenas, large herds of Cape buffalos, and African elephants grazing against the backdrop of the Rwenzori Mountains. Return to the lodge for a hearty midday lunch and some relaxation. In the afternoon, head to Mweya peninsula for a thrilling two-hour boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, a natural waterway connecting Lake George and Lake Edward. Drift past massive pods of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, bathing elephants, and extraordinary waterbirds including African skimmers, pelicans, and kingfishers. Conclude with a sunset game drive en route back to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Katwe Explosion Craters Exploration & Return Drive to Kampala

After breakfast, check out of the lodge and take a scenic tour of the Katwe explosion crater drive, offering breathtaking panoramic vistas of ancient volcanic calderas and the Great Rift Valley. Visit Lake Katwe to witness traditional salt mining methods that have been practiced by local communities for centuries. Afterward, begin your return journey back toward Kampala or Entebbe, pausing for lunch along the route. Arrive in the city by late afternoon or early evening, where your guide drops you off at your chosen hotel or Entebbe International Airport.
