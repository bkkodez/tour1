Explore Rwanda

The Land of a Thousand Hills

Known as the Land of a Thousand Hills, Rwanda is one of Africa’s most captivating eco-tourism destinations. From life-changing mountain gorilla tracking in the Virunga volcanic peaks and chimpanzee treks in ancient rainforests to classic savannah wildlife safaris and serene island-studded lakes, Rwanda offers extraordinary adventures crafted for discerning travellers.

Volcanoes National Park

Journey into Rwanda's volcanic highlands,where misty montain forests are home to endangered mountain gorillas and golden monkeys.Experience gorilla trekking,hiking and the remarkable landscapes of Virunga Massif

Explore Volcanoes

Akagera National Park

Explorer Rwanda's eastern savannah, where open plains,woodlands,wetlands and lakes create a spectacular wildlife experience. Discover the Big Five, Enjoy game drives and cruise the waters of Lake Ihema.

Explore Akagera

Nyungwe National Park

Step into one of Africa's oldest rainforests, rich in biodiversity and surrounded by spectacular mountain scenery.Encounter chimpazees and other primates, explore forest trails and experience the famous canopy walk.

Explore Nyungwe

Lake Kivu

Relax beside the beautifull waters of Lake Kivu,surrounded by mountains,islands and spectacular views. Enjoy a peaceful escape with opportunitiesfor kayaking,hiking, cycling and lakeside experiences.

Explore Lake Kivu
