Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Scenic Drive from Kampala/Entebbe to Bwindi Forest

Meet your Horizon Tours and Travel driver guide in the morning and commence the journey to southwestern Uganda. Stop at the Equator for photography and continue through Mbarara and the picturesque Kigezi highlands, often called the 'Switzerland of Africa'. Arrive in Bwindi in the late afternoon for dinner and rest.

DAY 02

Unforgettable Mountain Gorilla Trekking Experience

Following an early breakfast, report to the park headquarters for a briefing on gorilla etiquette and tracking guidelines. Led by expert UWA rangers and trackers, trek into the dense jungle foliage. Once you locate the gorilla family, spend a mesmerizing hour observing them feed, play, and interact before returning to the lodge.

DAY 03

Return Transfer to Entebbe / Kampala

After breakfast, begin your relaxed scenic drive back to Kampala or Entebbe. Enjoy a lunch stop en route and pause at cultural craft markets before arriving at your hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your evening departure.
