3 Day Uganda Gorilla Trekking

Embark on the ultimate wildlife pilgrimage into southwestern Uganda's ancient Bwindi Impenetrable National Park with Roots East Africa Adventure. Journey from Kampala or Entebbe across scenic undulating countryside, pausing at the iconic Uganda Equator and traveling through the terraced hills of the Kigezi highlands. Venture deep into the misty, primeval rainforest alongside expert Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers to encounter a habituated family of endangered mountain gorillas face-to-face. Spend an unforgettable hour in their presence, experience authentic indigenous Batwa heritage, and return with memories of a lifetime.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Scenic Drive from Kampala/Entebbe to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Your safari commences early as your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide meets you in Kampala or Entebbe. Journey southwest across Uganda’s scenic heartland, making an enjoyable first stop at the Equator crossing in Kayabwe for fascinating geographic demonstrations and photos. Continue through the undulating hills of Ankole, passing herds of iconic long-horned cattle, and pause in Mbarara for a traditional hot lunch. In the afternoon, the terrain transitions into the steep, terraced green mountains and mist-shrouded valleys of the Kigezi highlands. Arrive at Bwindi Impenetrable National Park in the late afternoon, check in to your lodge, and savor a peaceful dinner as twilight envelops the rainforest.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Rainforest & Batwa Cultural Experience

After an energizing breakfast, gather at the park visitor center for a comprehensive safety and conservation briefing by Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. You will be assigned to a habituated gorilla family and paired with experienced rangers and native trackers. Venture into the ancient, untamed jungle, following footprints, broken twigs, and nesting sites through dense foliage and misty ridges. The trek can take between 2 to 6 hours depending on the family's movement. When you make contact, all fatigue vanishes as you spend one unforgettable hour observing these magnificent primates in their natural sanctuary. Watch the dominant silverback watch over the troop, mothers cradle their young, and energetic juveniles play. In the late afternoon, enjoy an optional cultural encounter with the indigenous Batwa forest community to learn about their ancestral survival skills, medicinal plants, and traditional music before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Scenic Return Journey to Kampala or Entebbe

Enjoy a hearty breakfast with misty panoramic views of the forest canopy before beginning the return journey eastward. Travel along winding mountain passes through picturesque rural villages, stopping in Mbarara for lunch. Make another brief pause along the way at Mpambire drum makers village to witness traditional craftsmanship. Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe by late afternoon or evening, with drop-off at your designated hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your homeward connection.
