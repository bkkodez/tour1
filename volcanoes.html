Volcanoes National Park

Sanctuary of the Mountain Gorillas & the Dramatic Virunga Volcanoes

Plan Your Safari
Back to Rwanda Destinations

Volcanoes National Park (Parc National des Volcans) lies in northwestern Rwanda, protecting the steep slopes of the magnificent Virunga mountain range. Spanning a landscape of bamboo forests, mist-covered rainforests, and dramatic volcanic peaks, it is one of the world's most famous sanctuaries for endangered mountain gorillas and rare golden monkeys.

About Volcanoes National Park

Set against the backdrop of five dormant volcanoes — Karisimbi, Bisoke, Muhabura, Gahinga, and Sabyinyo — Volcanoes National Park is an extraordinary natural wonderland. It was here that legendary primatologist Dian Fossey based her groundbreaking conservation work with mountain gorillas.

The park's altitude ranges from 2,400 to over 4,500 metres, creating varied ecological zones from dense bamboo stands to alpine moorlands. This diverse habitat provides a rich haven for iconic primate species and exceptional afro-montane flora.

Volcanoes National Park is home to habituated mountain gorilla families and troops of endangered golden monkeys, making it Rwanda's premier primate trekking destination.

Key Highlights & Primates

The park offers intimate, world-class wildlife encounters that attract travellers from across the globe:

  • Mountain Gorillas — Encountering a gentle giant silverback in its natural bamboo habitat is widely regarded as one of Africa's most profound wildlife experiences.
  • Golden Monkeys — Playful, energetic, and distinctively marked with golden-orange fur, these rare primates leap effortlessly through the bamboo canopy.
  • Virunga Volcanic Peaks — Spectacular hiking trails ascend scenic volcanic summits, offering panoramic views across Rwanda, Uganda, and the DR Congo.

Major Activities

Unforgettable adventures in the Virunga highlands

Gorilla Trekking

Trek through scenic bamboo forests to spend an unforgettable hour in the presence of wild mountain gorillas.

Golden Monkey Tracking

Follow expert trackers into the bamboo zone to observe lively troops of endangered golden monkeys.

Volcano Hiking

Hike the scenic slopes of Mount Bisoke featuring a crater lake at its summit or trek Mount Karisimbi.

Wildlife Photography

Capture dramatic landscape vistas, lush misty ridges, and extraordinary close-up primate portraits.

Prominent Wildlife

Iconic animals of Volcanoes National Park

Mountain Gorillas

Majestic great apes thriving in protected family groups throughout the Virunga volcanic slopes.

Golden Monkeys

Endangered primates known for their vibrant golden coats and playful social interactions in the bamboo forest.
