Nyungwe National Park

Ancient Montane Rainforest, Chimpanzees & Suspended Canopy Walkways

Plan Your Safari
Back to Rwanda Destinations

Nyungwe National Park is one of Africa’s oldest and most biodiverse tropical rainforests, blanketing over 1,000 square kilometres of majestic mountain slopes in southwestern Rwanda. Famed for its thriving chimpanzee communities, large troops of Angolan colobus monkeys, and iconic canopy suspension walkway, Nyungwe is a must-visit primate sanctuary.

About Nyungwe National Park

Surviving since the last Ice Age, Nyungwe feeds water into both the Congo River Basin and the Nile River Basin. Its dense, mist-enshrouded canopy reaches elevations between 1,600 and nearly 3,000 metres above sea level, preserving thousands of plant species, wild orchids, and century-old mahogany trees.

Nyungwe holds 13 primate species, making it a critical biodiversity stronghold in the Albertine Rift. Beyond primate tracking, visitors can explore serene tea plantations on the forest periphery and trek through scenic river valleys and waterfalls.

Nyungwe is home to wild chimpanzees and large super-troops of black-and-white colobus monkeys, alongside East Africa's only canopy suspension bridge.

Key Highlights & Ancient Forest Ecology

Nyungwe offers rich immersive experiences for wildlife and nature enthusiasts:

  • Chimpanzee Communities — Trek into the ancient forest at dawn to observe chimpanzees feeding, grooming, and vocalizing high in the trees.
  • Angolan Colobus Troops — Witness mega-troops numbering hundreds of colobus monkeys leaping gracefully through the forest branches.
  • Suspended Canopy Walkway — A 160-metre-long walkway suspended 70 metres above the forest floor offering eye-level views of birds, primates, and giant trees.

Major Activities

Primate tracking and scenic rainforest adventures

Chimpanzee Trekking

Set off with expert guides into Cyamudongo or Uwinka forest sectors to encounter habituated chimpanzees in their natural habitat.

Canopy Walk

Cross the thrilling suspended canopy walkway high above the deep rainforest valley for spectacular panoramic vistas.

Colobus Monkey Tracking

Follow dedicated nature trails to track and photograph playful troops of acrobatic Angolan colobus monkeys.

Waterfall Hikes

Hike through giant tree ferns and lush mossy glades along the Isumo Waterfall trail to a thundering rainforest cascade.

Prominent Wildlife

Remarkable primates of Nyungwe National Park

Chimpanzees

Highly intelligent great apes living in vibrant social communities across Nyungwe's montane rainforest.

Colobus Monkeys & Birdlife

Black-and-white primates and rare endemic bird species celebrated throughout the forest.
