6 Days Uganda Gorilla Trekking Safari in Uganda from Kigali

Take advantage of the shortest and most scenic route into Uganda's premier wildlife sanctuaries on this exceptional 6-day safari starting and ending in Kigali, Rwanda. By arriving into Kigali and crossing directly into southwestern Uganda, you dramatically reduce overland road travel while combining two world-class national parks: tracking wild mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, experiencing classic savannah wildlife, tree-climbing lions, and a Kazinga Channel boat safari in Queen Elizabeth National Park, and unwinding along the picturesque island shores of Lake Bunyonyi.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 â€” Kigali Pickup & Scenic Cross-Border Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Meet your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide in Kigali, either upon arrival at Kigali International Airport or at your city hotel. After an orientation briefing, take an enjoyable city tour visiting the Kigali Genocide Memorial before driving northward through Rwanda's scenic terraced hills. Cross into southwestern Uganda via the Cyanika or Katuna border post and climb into the spectacular Kigezi highlands. Arrive at your lodge on the periphery of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park in the afternoon, check in, and enjoy dinner while preparing for your gorilla trek.

DAY 02

Day 2 â€” Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Rainforest & Batwa Community Visit

Following an early breakfast, gather at the park headquarters for a vital briefing on gorilla etiquette and tracking safety from Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. Venturing into the ancient misty jungle with expert trackers, search for your assigned habituated gorilla family. Trekking duration ranges from 2 to 6 hours over rugged terrain. Once located, spend an extraordinary hour in the presence of these gentle giantsâ€”observing the massive silverback, playful youngsters, and caring mothers. In the late afternoon, enjoy an optional cultural visit to the indigenous Batwa community before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 â€” Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions

After breakfast, depart Bwindi and head north toward Queen Elizabeth National Park. Enter through the southern Ishasha sector, celebrated worldwide for its unique population of tree-climbing lions. Embark on a game drive across the savannah, scanning the branches of giant sycamore fig trees for lions lounging above ground, alongside herds of topis, Uganda kobs, and buffalos. Enjoy lunch en route and continue to the central sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park, checking in to your safari lodge for dinner and overnight rest.

DAY 04

Day 4 â€” Kasenyi Savannah Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Set out at dawn onto the Kasenyi plains for an exhilarating morning game drive. Track lions, leopards, spotted hyenas, large herds of elephants, and Cape buffalos in the crisp morning air with the Rwenzori Mountains as your backdrop. Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation. In the afternoon, embark on the renowned two-hour boat safari along the Kazinga Channel, a natural waterway connecting Lake George and Lake Edward. Drift past massive pods of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, bathing elephants, and extraordinary flocks of waterbirds. Return to the lodge for dinner.

DAY 05

Day 5 â€” Scenic Explosion Crater Lakes Drive & Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi

After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive through the Katwe explosion crater lakes area, offering breathtaking panorama vistas of ancient volcanic craters and the Rwenzori mountain ranges. Depart Queen Elizabeth National Park and travel southward toward serene Lake Bunyonyiâ€”one of Africa's deepest and most tranquil crater lakes, framed by 29 lush islands. Arrive at your lakeside eco-lodge in time for lunch. In the afternoon, set out on a motorized boat trip across the lake visiting historical islands such as Akampene (Punishment Island) and Bwama Island, or simply relax at the water's edge enjoying the calm mountain scenery.

DAY 06

Day 6 â€” Lake Bunyonyi Sunrise Relaxation & Scenic Return Transfer to Kigali

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast on the terrace overlooking the tranquil, mist-shrouded waters of Lake Bunyonyi. Check out and commence your scenic return road trip across the border back into Rwanda via Cyanika or Katuna. Marvel at the dramatic rolling hills of Rwanda as you drive to Kigali, arriving in time for lunch. Spend the afternoon browsing Kigali's artisan craft markets for souvenirs before your driver guide drops you off at Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
