5 Days Uganda Primate Safari

Immerse yourself in Uganda's extraordinary primate realm on this premier 5-day safari focusing on Africa’s most sought-after great apes. Journey to Kibale Forest National Park—the Primate Capital of the World—boasting 13 distinct primate species and the highest concentration of habituated wild chimpanzees in East Africa. Continue through southwest Uganda’s dramatic landscapes to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a once-in-a-lifetime mountain gorilla trekking expedition, creating the ultimate primate double-encounter in their virgin equatorial rainforest habitats.

Key Experiences

Safari Highlights

  • Day 1:

    Scenic drive from Entebbe/Kampala to Kibale Forest and afternoon Bigodi Wetland guided eco-walk.

  • Day 2:

    Exciting chimpanzee tracking in Kibale Forest and scenic transfer toward the Bwindi highlands.

  • Day 3:

    Arrival in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park with an afternoon Batwa cultural community experience.

  • Day 4:

    Unforgettable mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Rainforest and evening fireside relaxation.

  • Day 5:

    Return journey to Entebbe/Kampala featuring a stopover at the famous Uganda Equator monument.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Entebbe/Kampala to Kibale Forest National Park & Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary

Your safari begins with an early morning departure from your Entebbe hotel or residence in Kampala. Embark on a scenic westward drive passing bustling market towns, green papyrus swamps, and the rolling tea plantations surrounding the picturesque town of Fort Portal beneath the snow-capped Rwenzori Mountains. Arrive at Kibale Forest National Park in time for lunch and lodge check-in. In the afternoon, enjoy a guided nature walk through Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary—a community-managed biodiversity hotspot renowned for eight primate species including red colobus monkeys, black-and-white colobus, grey-cheeked mangabeys, and over 200 bird species including the iconic Great Blue Turaco. Return to your lodge for dinner and overnight rest.

DAY 02

Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest & Transfer toward Bwindi Region

Gather at the Kanyanchu Visitor Centre after breakfast for a comprehensive briefing from Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. Head deep into the tropical rainforest to track one of Kibale's habituated chimpanzee communities. Follow their lively calls and tree-drumming reverberating across the canopy. Upon locating the troop, spend an exhilarating hour observing our closest living relatives as they feed, groom, groom infants, and traverse the forest floor. After the trek, return for a well-deserved hot lunch before embarking on a scenic southward drive along the edge of Queen Elizabeth National Park toward the Bwindi highlands. Arrive at your transition lodge in the late afternoon.

DAY 03

Scenic Drive into Bwindi Impenetrable National Park & Batwa Cultural Tour

After breakfast, wind your way into the dramatic misty ridges of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. This UNESCO World Heritage site is one of Africa’s most ancient and biologically diverse ecosystems, dating back more than 25,000 years. Check into your rainforest lodge and savor lunch with breathtaking views over the emerald jungle canopy. In the afternoon, participate in an authentic Batwa Pygmy community cultural walk. The Batwa were original forest dwellers who lived harmoniously inside Bwindi before conservation gazetting. Discover their traditional hunting lore, fire-making techniques, medicinal plants, and traditional music and dance. Spend the evening resting in preparation for tomorrow's gorilla trek.

DAY 04

Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

The highlight of your Uganda primate safari. Following an early breakfast, arrive at the UWA park headquarters for registration and a briefing on gorilla trekking guidelines. Divided into small groups of eight, accompanied by armed rangers and knowledgeable trackers, enter Bwindi’s tangled undergrowth. The trek traverses steep valleys and rainforest slopes until you finally encounter your allocated mountain gorilla family. Standing just a few meters away from a magnificent silverback and watching playful juveniles interact is a deeply moving wildlife experience that few travelers ever forget. After your permitted one hour with the gentle giants, trek back to base for your official gorilla trekking certificate before returning to your lodge for relaxation and dinner.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Packed Lunch & Dinner

DAY 05

Return to Entebbe / Kampala with Uganda Equator Stopover

Enjoy your final breakfast surrounded by Bwindi’s misty mountains before beginning your return journey eastward. Travel through the scenic terraced hills of Kigezi and the sprawling savannah ranchlands of Ankole, famous for long-horned cattle. Break for lunch in Mbarara, then continue to the Uganda Equator monument in Kayabwe. Stand with one foot in the Northern Hemisphere and the other in the Southern Hemisphere, witness the fascinating Coriolis effect water experiment, and browse local handicraft shops. Continue to Entebbe or Kampala, arriving in the late afternoon or early evening for drop-off at your hotel or transfer to Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight.

Meal Plan: Breakfast & Lunch

Drop-off: Entebbe International Airport or Kampala Hotel
