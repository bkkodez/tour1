5 Days Uganda Primate Safari

Immerse yourself in Uganda's extraordinary primate realm on this premier 5-day safari focusing on Africa’s most sought-after great apes. Journey to Kibale Forest National Park—the Primate Capital of the World—boasting 13 distinct primate species and the highest concentration of habituated wild chimpanzees in East Africa. Continue through southwest Uganda’s dramatic landscapes to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a once-in-a-lifetime mountain gorilla trekking expedition, creating the ultimate primate double-encounter in their virgin equatorial rainforest habitats.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

Your primate expedition commences with an early morning departure from your hotel in Entebbe or residence in Kampala by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide. Travel westward across Uganda's scenic countryside, passing vibrant market towns, lush wetlands, and sprawling tea plantations surrounding Fort Portal beneath the dramatic foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains. Arrive at Kibale Forest National Parkâ€”the Primate Capital of the Worldâ€”in time for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on a guided eco-walk through Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, a community-run conservation reserve home to eight primate species including red colobus, black-and-white colobus, and grey-cheeked mangabeys, alongside more than 200 species of birds such as the magnificent Great Blue Turaco. Return to your lodge for dinner and a restful evening.

DAY 02

Day 2 â€” Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest & Scenic Transfer toward the Bwindi Region

After breakfast, gather at Kanyanchu Visitor Center for an informative briefing by Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers before entering the ancient equatorial rainforest. Kibale boasts over 1,500 wild chimpanzees. Follow skilled trackers through the dense foliage, listening for whoops, pant-hoots, and tree-drumming echoing across the canopy. Once you encounter the chimpanzee troop, spend a thrilling hour watching them forage, groom, care for infants, and swing gracefully through the treetops. Following the trek, return to the lodge for lunch, then embark on a picturesque journey southward along the edge of Queen Elizabeth National Park toward the highland gateway of Bwindi, arriving at your lodge in time for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 â€” Journey into Bwindi Impenetrable National Park & Batwa Cultural Community Experience

Savor breakfast with expansive views over the misty mountains before continuing deeper into the ancient jungle of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. A UNESCO World Heritage site dating back over 25,000 years, Bwindi holds nearly half of the world's surviving mountain gorillas. Check in to your forest lodge and enjoy a leisurely lunch overlooking the emerald canopy. In the afternoon, take part in an authentic Batwa cultural experience. Learn firsthand from the original forest-dwellers about their ancestral hunting techniques, medicinal forest herbs, traditional fire-making, and ancient folklore, concluding with traditional songs and dances. Return to your lodge to rest and prepare for tomorrow's gorilla trek.

DAY 04

Day 4 â€” Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest & Evening Fireside Relaxation

Rise early for a nutritious breakfast, collect your packed lunch, and transfer to the park headquarters for a vital pre-trek briefing. Accompanied by experienced UWA rangers and armed scouts, venture into the tangled undergrowth and steep mountain ridges. Tracking can take anywhere from 2 to 6 hours depending on the movement of the gorilla family. Finding yourself face-to-face with a majestic silverback and his troop is one of the world's most powerful wildlife encounters. Spend an unforgettable hour observing their gentle nature, family bonds, and playful youngsters. Hike back to the base to receive your official gorilla trekking certificate before returning to your lodge for dinner and fireside storytelling under the African stars.

DAY 05

Day 5 â€” Return Journey to Entebbe/Kampala with a Stopover at the Uganda Equator Monument

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast amidst the sounds of the waking rainforest before commencing your journey back to Kampala or Entebbe. Travel through the terraced green hills of Kigezi and the scenic ranchlands of Ankole, pausing in Mbarara for lunch. Along the highway, make a stop at the famous Uganda Equator line in Kayabwe to take photos, watch the Coriolis water demonstration, and browse local handicraft markets. Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon or early evening, where your guide drops you off at your hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight.
