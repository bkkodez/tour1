Gorilla Trekking Safari in Uganda from Kigali

Experience the ultimate 7-day East African great ape and wildlife safari conveniently beginning and concluding in Kigali, Rwanda, with Roots East Africa Adventure. Journey seamlessly across the border into southwestern Uganda's ancient Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a life-defining encounter with endangered mountain gorillas. Travel northward through the Ishasha sector to track legendary tree-climbing lions, venture across the savannahs of Queen Elizabeth National Park on sunrise game drives, cruise the wildlife-rich Kazinga Channel amidst hippos and Nile crocodiles, and track habituated wild chimpanzees in the equatorial rainforest of Kibale Forest National Park.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Pickup in Kigali, City Tour & Scenic Cross-Border Transfer to Bwindi Forest

Your safari begins with a morning pickup from your hotel or Kigali International Airport by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide. Start with a brief orientation tour of Kigali, including a meaningful visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial. Enjoy lunch at a traditional restaurant before setting out on a scenic drive northward through Rwanda's rolling emerald hills. Cross the border into southwestern Uganda via Cyanika or Katuna and ascend into the dramatic, terraced Kigezi highlands. Arrive at your lodge bordering Bwindi Impenetrable National Park in the afternoon to settle in, enjoy dinner, and rest ahead of tomorrow's gorilla trek.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest & Batwa Community Visit

Wake early for breakfast, collect your packed lunch, and transfer to the park headquarters for a thorough briefing on gorilla trekking guidelines from Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers. In small groups accompanied by expert trackers, venture into the ancient, dense rainforest. Hike along steep ridges and tangled jungle paths until you make contact with your assigned gorilla family. Spend an awe-inspiring hour observing them at close quarters — watching mothers nurture their young, juveniles romp through the vines, and the mighty silverback keep watchful guard. In the afternoon, enjoy an authentic Batwa cultural community encounter before returning to the lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Ishasha Tree-Climbing Lions

After breakfast, depart Bwindi and head north toward Queen Elizabeth National Park. Enter through the southern Ishasha sector, world-renowned for its rare population of tree-climbing lions. Embark on a game drive across the savannah, scanning the expansive branches of giant sycamore fig trees for lions lounging to escape the midday heat, alongside topis, kobs, and buffalos. Enjoy a picnic or en-route lunch and continue to the central sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park, checking in to your safari lodge for dinner and an overnight stay overlooking the plains.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Kasenyi Savannah Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Safari

Begin your morning at first light with an exhilarating game drive across the Kasenyi plains. Track lions, leopards, spotted hyenas, large herds of elephants, and Cape buffalos against the majestic backdrop of the Rwenzori Mountains. Return to the lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, embark on the legendary two-hour boat safari along the Kazinga Channel, a natural waterway connecting Lake George and Lake Edward. Glide within meters of massive schools of hippos, basking Nile crocodiles, bathing elephants, and hundreds of species of vibrant waterbirds. Return to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Transfer to Kibale Forest National Park & Bigodi Wetland Eco-Walk

After breakfast, take a scenic drive north toward Kibale Forest National Park, passing scenic crater lakes and lush tea estates near Fort Portal. Check into your lodge nestled in the rainforest and enjoy lunch. In the afternoon, head to Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary for a guided eco-walk. Accompanied by knowledgeable community guides, discover this biodiversity hotspot home to eight primate species — including red colobus and grey-cheeked mangabeys — and over 200 bird species such as the Great Blue Turaco. Return to your lodge for dinner and relaxation.

DAY 06

Day 6 — Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest & Crater Lakes Exploration

Gather at the Kanyanchu Visitor Center after breakfast for registration and a briefing on chimpanzee tracking protocols. Guided by UWA rangers, trek into the equatorial rainforest following vocal calls and tree-drumming to locate a habituated chimpanzee community. Spend a thrilling hour watching them feed, play, and swing through the canopy. Following the trek, enjoy lunch at your lodge. In the afternoon, take an excursion through the breathtaking Ndali-Kasenda explosion crater lakes area, taking in panoramic vistas of ancient caldera lakes and rural tea plantations. Dinner and overnight at your lodge.

DAY 07

Day 7 — Scenic Return Journey Across the Border to Kigali & Departure Flight

Enjoy breakfast amidst the gentle morning sounds of the forest before beginning your return journey southward toward Rwanda. Cross the border via Katuna or Cyanika and wind through Rwanda's magnificent highland countryside, arriving in Kigali in time for lunch. Spend the afternoon browsing local artisan craft centers for souvenirs before your driver guide drops you off at Kigali International Airport for your evening flight departure.
