Gorilla Trekking Safari in Uganda from Kigali

An extraordinary journey across western Uganda's premier national parks, beginning and ending conveniently from Kigali. Experience the thunder of Murchison Falls, habituated chimpanzee tracking in Kibale Forest, classic savannah wildlife in Queen Elizabeth, and tranquil crater lake landscapes.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Transfer to Murchison Falls via Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

Depart Kampala heading north to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for an on-foot white rhino tracking experience. Continue to Murchison Falls National Park and visit the top of the thunderous falls before checking into your lodge for dinner.

DAY 02

Murchison Delta Game Drive & Nile Boat Safari

Embark on an early morning game drive along the delta tracks spotting lions, giraffes, elephants, and antelopes. In the afternoon, cruise up the River Nile to the foot of Murchison Falls, marveling at the wall of water and abundant riverbank wildlife.

DAY 03

Scenic Drive to Kibale Forest National Park

Enjoy a scenic drive southward passing through tea plantations and traditional homesteads in the shadow of the Rwenzori Mountains. Arrive at Fort Portal and settle into your forest lodge near Kibale National Park.

DAY 04

Chimpanzee Tracking & Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary Walk

Trek into Kibale rainforest in search of our closest living relatives, the chimpanzees. Watch them swing through the canopy and communicate with vocal pant-hoots. In the afternoon, enjoy a guided community swamp walk in Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary.

DAY 05

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park & Afternoon Boat Cruise

Drive south across the Equator to Queen Elizabeth National Park. In the afternoon, embark on the world-famous boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, observing massive pods of hippos, bathing elephants, and diverse bird species.

DAY 06

Morning Game Safari & Kasenyi Plains Exploration

Rise for an early morning game drive across the Kasenyi savannah plains searching for lions on the hunt, leopards, Uganda kobs, and giant forest hogs. Spend the afternoon exploring picturesque volcanic crater lakes.

DAY 07

Return Transfer to Kampala or Entebbe

Enjoy breakfast at your lodge before beginning your journey back to Kampala or Entebbe. Make a stop at the Equator monument for lunch and shopping, concluding an unforgettable safari.
