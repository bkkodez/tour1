11 Days Best of Uganda Safari

The ultimate Grand Uganda Safari. Covering the Pearl of Africa's absolute finest highlights: on-foot rhino tracking at Ziwa, the thunder of Murchison Falls, chimpanzee tracking in Kibale, classic big game in Queen Elizabeth, mountain gorillas in Bwindi, and island canoeing on Lake Bunyonyi.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival in Kigali and City Tour.

When you arrive at Kigali International Airport, meet your safari guide from Bora Safaris and begin exploring the country's vibrant capital. Visit selected cultural and historical landmarks to discover Rwanda's heritage and exceptional transformation before transferring to your hotel for a relaxing evening.

DAY 02

Transfer to Volcanoes National Park.

Following breakfast, travel north toward Volcanoes National Park, passing through Rwanda's picturesque countryside and rolling green hills. As you approach Musanze, enjoy spectacular views of the volcanic mountains that form part of the Virunga range. Arrive at your lodge, settle in and prepare for the following day's mountain gorilla trekking adventure

DAY 03

Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Rwanda.

Experience one of Africa's most extraordinary wildlife encounters as you enter Volcanoes National Park with experienced rangers and trackers. Follow forest trails while looking for a habituated mountain gorilla family and discover the beauty of this unique mountain ecosystem. After finding the gorillas, spend one unforgettable hour observing their behaviour and family interactions before returning to your lodge. In the evening explore the guardian village of Ib’iwachu, where you will meet rehabilitated poacher who will put on performances exhibiting Rwanda’s traditional cultures. After lunch, transfer to Queen Elizabeth national park to reduce your distance for the next day transfer. It’s a 3 hours’ drive from Kibale national park to Queen Elizabeth national park. Dinner and overnight at Elephant Hub Lodge for mid-range.

DAY 04

Golden Monkey Tracking and Transfer to Bwindi, Uganda.

Following breakfast, proceed to Volcano NP offices for briefing where you will be assigned to an experienced rangers to lead to on the look for the Golden monkeys in the bamboo forests of Volcanoes National Park. Watch these lively primates move through the vegetation before returning to prepare for your cross-border journey into Uganda. Continue toward Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, enjoying the scenic highlands along the way, and register at your lodge.

DAY 05

Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi.

Have breakfast and go to Bwindi station for briefing where you are allocated a Gorilla family to track and assigned with an experienced ranget to take you on the traking. Follow forest trails through dense vegetation while discovering the exceptional ecosystem these primates call home. After finding the gorillas, spend one precious hour observing their behavior, family interactions and daily activities from a respectful distance before returning to your lodge. In the afternoon, you may go on a guided village tour to meet the Batwa tribe who previously lived in the Forrest and were settled into the community. You can also go for a coffee tour or visit a homestead, school, or hospital.

DAY 06

Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi.

Following breakfast, journey to the striking Lake Bunyonyi, celebrated for its tranquil waters, terraced hillsides and picturesque islands. The scenic drive offers excellent opportunities to appreciate the landscapes and rural life of southwestern Uganda. On arrival, settle into your accommodation and spend the remainder of the day enjoying the lakeside atmosphere. After settling in embark on a boat excursion to visit some of the islands on L. Bunyonyi including Punishment Island where in the past young girls were dumped for comiteeing fornication/ adultary.

DAY 07

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Leave Lake Bunyonyi and continue toward Queen Elizabeth National Park, travelling through the rolling hills and rural communities of western Uganda. Take in the changing landscapes as you approach one of the country's leading wildlife destinations. On arrival, register at your lodge and enjoy a relaxing evening in preparation for your safari activities.

DAY 08

Game Drive and Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise.

Begin your morning with a wildlife drive across Queen Elizabeth National Park's open plains while looking for elephants, buffaloes, Uganda kob, antelopes and lions. After lunch, embark on a scenic boat excursion along the Kazinga Channel, famous for its large hippo populations, crocodiles and abundant birdlife. Watch wildlife gather along the shoreline while enjoying excellent opportunities for photography.

DAY 09

Transfer to Kibale Forest National Park.

Following breakfast, journey to Kibale Forest National Park, a renowned destination for chimpanzees and other primates. Take in the scenic countryside, crater lakes and lush vegetation of western Uganda along the way. Arrive at your lodge near the forest, settle in and relax ahead of your chimpanzee tracking experience.

DAY 10

Chimpanzee Tracking in Kibale Forest.

Venture into Kibale Forest with experienced guides for an exciting look for wild chimpanzees. Follow forest trails, listen for their distinctive calls and observe their behaviour when you encounter them. Discover the rich biodiversity of the rainforest, which is also home to several other primate species and colourful birds, before returning to your lodge.

DAY 11

Transfer to Entebbe and Departure.

Following breakfast, begin your return journey toward Entebbe, passing through the scenic landscapes of western and central Uganda. Take in the final views of the countryside before arriving at Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight. Your adventure concludes with unforgettable memories of two countries, two gorilla trekking experiences and Uganda's exceptional wildlife.
