5 Days Rwanda–Uganda Gorilla Trekking Tour

Experience the ultimate double gorilla trekking adventure across two premier East African destinations. Journey through Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park and Uganda's UNESCO-listed Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, comparing the distinct volcanic slopes and ancient rainforest habitats of the endangered mountain gorilla. Accompanied by expert rangers, enjoy two profound face-to-face encounters with habituated gorilla families, alongside golden monkey tracking and authentic cultural heritage encounters.

Day by Day Exploration

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 01

Day 1 — Arrival in Kigali, City Tour & Scenic Drive to Volcanoes National Park

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you are warmly welcomed by your Roots East Africa Adventure driver guide. Begin with a guided orientation tour of Kigali, including an insightful and moving visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial. Enjoy lunch at a traditional city restaurant before embarking on a scenic two-and-a-half-hour drive northwest through Rwanda's famously terraced, emerald green hillsides. Arrive in the town of Musanze at the base of the majestic Virunga Volcanoes, check in to your lodge, and enjoy dinner while briefing on the exhilarating days ahead.

DAY 02

Day 2 — Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Volcanoes National Park & Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village

Rise early for breakfast and head to the Kinigi park headquarters for registration and an essential pre-trek briefing with park rangers. In small groups accompanied by expert trackers, hike up the volcanic slopes through bamboo forest to locate your habituated gorilla family. Trekking times range from 1 to 5 hours depending on the troop's movement. Once you encounter them, spend a breathtaking, deeply moving hour observing the gentle giants feed, play, and interact in their natural habitat. In the afternoon, visit the Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village (Gorilla Guardians) to experience traditional Rwandan dance, drumming, archery, and royal customs before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 03

Day 3 — Golden Monkey Tracking & Scenic Cross-Border Transfer to Bwindi Forest

After breakfast, return to Kinigi for a morning trek tracking the endangered, highly energetic golden monkeys in the bamboo canopy of Volcanoes National Park. Watch them leap gracefully through the branches and forage along the forest floor. Return to the lodge to freshen up and enjoy lunch before commencing your scenic road journey crossing the border into southwestern Uganda via the Cyanika border post. As you enter Uganda, climb through the dramatic terraced highlands of Kigezi, arriving at your lodge nestled on the edge of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for dinner and overnight rest.

DAY 04

Day 4 — Second Gorilla Trek in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park & Batwa Cultural Visit

Today brings your second mountain gorilla encounter, offering a unique opportunity to contrast gorilla trekking in Uganda's ancient, dense rainforest against yesterday's volcanic slopes. Following a briefing at UWA park headquarters, trek into Bwindi's primordial jungle led by experienced rangers and trackers. Spend an unforgettable hour with another gorilla family, observing distinct group behaviors, playful youngsters, and a commanding silverback. Afterward, receive your trekking certificate. In the late afternoon, enjoy an authentic cultural encounter with the indigenous Batwa community, discovering their traditional forest lore, survival skills, and music before returning to your lodge for dinner.

DAY 05

Day 5 — Scenic Return Journey Across the Border to Kigali & Departure Flight

Savor breakfast with sweeping views of the misty Bwindi forest before beginning your return journey southward across the border into Rwanda via Cyanika or Katuna. Wind through the spectacular rolling countryside, arriving in Kigali in time for lunch. Spend the afternoon browsing local artisan craft centers for handmade baskets and souvenirs, or enjoying coffee at a local café, before your driver guide transfers you to Kigali International Airport for your evening flight departure.
